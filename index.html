Campaign

This website is dedicated to the two and a half year campaign that I wrote for my friends.

It takes place in the fictional land of Elustria; An empire once made up of warring states and houses, now under the unifying rule of King Barrett the Bloody. Although there have been no major conflicts for the past 100 years, a nation of such a size required more care than it could provide; Especially in regards to crime. To strengthen their policing efforts, the empire created CROWN, a nationally-funded bounty hunting guild. Any no-named adventurer could seek fortune and fame by collecting quarryies for the Elustrian Empire.

Cut to a small tavern in the town of Drachenburg. A group of bounty hunters coincidentally meet while hunting for the same bounties. After succesfully teaming up to hunt them down, they travel together to turn in the heads and earn their reward. As they arrive to the CROWN guild office, a new bounty is placed on the board; One that will shake up the nation. A ten million platinum reward, the highest bounty in Elustria's history, for a man named Melville Barrett.

Unsure as to their connection to the royal family, with no information given on the crimes committed, The Boys band together to seek this Melville Barrett; Uncovering the darker side of this peaceful nation.
