<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 10</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">As The Boys enter the city proper for the first time, enamored by the cultural shift from the outskirts to the inner city, they hear a town crier yell some interesting news. Much regarding the goings on of the city: The Librarians are in town burning literature, the Humming Oath are putting up flyers illegally, and Boggle Ball season is coming soon. Some of the news involved past actions of the party, but the one of main concern to The Boys was that the Pearl Legion are looking for those who attacked the slave caravan that Artie had been a part of a couple weeks ago. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The group decided to head over to The Lunar Tea Shop Theater to look into this show they had been hearing about, as well as the Gem of Barronia; a prostitute of renown in Elustria. Once they arrived at the Bulwark District where most of the tourism resides, they noticed that many passersby were uncomfortable with Artie. After some failed intimidation tactics, the group was approached by a pair of Night Guards. After informing the party members that all Druid “slaves” must be kept on a leash at all times in Barronia, Veyron threatened to cut off their balls. They walked away with a warning, for now.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">nce they arrived at the theater, most of the group went inside to check in about tickets. However, Timble went around back and met his first customer of Barronia. An actor at the theater named Triark bought the rest of Mivors Dort’s stash as well as the hallucina-voyant pedals. After making the deal, Timble was able to snatch the party some tickets to Mr. Queen’s new play. Those who went into the theater met the ticket master, Haido, and learned that Mr. Queen hadn’t actually been around the theater in about a week, missing out on tech rehearsal. Mordos also inquired about Lady Mirathel, the Gem of Barronia, and how she was trying to move away from her prostitution work and towards performing arts. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After leaving the theater, the group split up. Mordos and Veyron went across the plaza to visit The Squire and Warden; Lady Mirathel’s main place of employment. The rest of the group went over to The Artisanal Rooster to deliver the flowers to Bulder Ross. Once they delivered the flowers, they received the Revivification Elixir; A one-time use potion that will bring someone back from the dead a minute after their passing. After having concluded their transaction, Builder asked Angus about their home. It was a cryptic conversation that revealed that the curse that affects both of these fairies begins to erase one’s memory if they stay in Elustria for too long.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Back at The Squire and Warden, Mordos and Veyron met the Lady of the House, Cyranthess Baequiathem. They asked for Lady Mirathel, only to be struck down. It seems that the Gem of Barronia doesn’t just see anyone, and even then the price is steep. Veyron tries to get in by showing her the pentagram he had nabbed off of an Oni Clan member’s corpse. After pleading desperately and even asking if they had BIRDS at the establishment, the boys were content with using what little money they had left to buy some company for the afternoon. The two bachelors ended up being teleported to separate rooms. Barely decorated with no windows and a creepy statue in the corner. After doing some investigating, Veyron realized that these statues had been animated and were watching their every move. They got under the covers to hide the fact that they were asking for the following information:</p>
<p>-There are many rumors about Melville Barrett. Some say that he is the king’s uncle, other’s say an illegitimate son vying for the crown. Regardless of the story, it all seems like baseless gossip amongst bounty hunters.</p>
<p>-Some of the workers have heard that the Butterfly Killer only targets wealthy and powerful people on the road.</p>
<p>-Lady Mirathel started out young there and seemed to gain popularity out of nowhere. Now she tangles with nobles and merchants, and there are rumors that she might be a Druid.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before Mordos leaves the brothel, he asks his companion if she knew about any Druid coven nearby as a follow up to Emiko’s last message. She not only didn’t know anything, but seemed to be disgusted at the thought of it. Mordos responded back to Emiko via sending stone: “That’s very interesting, where can I find them? It may be helpful for Artie. . . Love you. . .” No response yet.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Boys spend a good part of their first day shopping at the many wonderful shops of Barronia. While most had an uneventful shopping spree, Hack had another strange encounter with his cursed blade. The owner of the weapon store, Kaito, offered to give him a discount if he could inspect the cutlass he had found after defeating the Plaguebringers. When Hack reached for the blade, it whispered to him once more. Not only was he unable to hand him the blade for a moment, but it asked him to kill the shopkeeper. Hack refused the demand once more. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After the party was done spending all of their hard earned gold, they split off into separate groups to follow up on loose ends.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Timble and Mordos ventured off to deliver the letter they had gotten off of Zusha near the Red Ravens’ hideout a few days ago. They arrived at the Tipsy Troll Inn and asked for Mike Ontact, claiming to be his cousins. They met the busboy out back and delivered the letter that was entrusted to them, as well as “confirming” Zusha’s death to his coworker. After a brief conversation, the two manage to get a meeting with his boss, Eddie Zolxarx, about the possibility of shady work for the party. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Zellicek and Veyron followed up on the lead they had gotten from the two boys carving secret messages into a tree outside of town. They wound up at The Sundering Leopard, a run down restaurant near the royal highway into Barronia, where they meet a rude waiter. After taking some time to discuss whether to dine inside or by a window, they abruptly ask him about the secret messages. He claims to not know anything, and as he leaves out the back door of the establishment, Pooter follows him. They eventually wind up at a park filled with different kinds of tents aptly named Tentvilla. They manage to track down the waiter into a small tent, but when they enter the space it ends up being much bigger than it appears from the outside. All that is inside is a tunnel burrowed into the ground. They follow it and end up on a beach just south of Barronia; No waiter in sight. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Angus and Hack brought Edwin Florgelsports’ deed to a Boggle Ball franchise to the NBBA Barronia HQ in hopes of becoming the new owners of the recently murdered proprietor’s team. After laying down some charm on the ugly aarakocra receptionist, the two of them got a meeting with Edwin’s personal assistant, Issabella. When they arrived with the news of his murder, she seemed unaware of what had happened. They lied to her about their encounter with Edwin; Claiming to have made good friends with him before his death, leading to a cosigning of his new Avilon team, The Sea Shanties. Without seeing either of their signatures on any of the papers they had returned to her, she was skeptical. She told them they should return in a few days after she was able to speak to Mrs. Florgelsports and take a look at his will. To Hack and Angus’s surprise, Issabella had mentioned that sometimes when there’s a dispute post mortem, they can hire professionals to speak to the dead to clear things up. They left the NBBA offices with a lot to think about.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The group meets back up just in time for the play, From Kids to Kings. After watching the first of supposedly many other short plays, the event is canceled as a group of Librarians walk on stage and announce that everyone vacates the premises. They are now in search of lodging for the night. </p>
