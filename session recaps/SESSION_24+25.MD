<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 24 & 25</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Red Raven Pirates finally arrived and picked up the party near the freshly abandoned camp grounds. They made their way across the monster infested waters, past the Connecting Isles. On their first day at sea, they witnessed a ship wreck not far from them. There were men swimming and floating on pieces of driftwood, as well as many crates of mystery cargo. The ship was divided on what to do. The tension between Crowley and his first mate was noticeable to the party. They fished out a crate or two and left the sailors to their fate. The next  day they encountered a solitary raft carrying a familiar face; Featherbottom, the blind kenku sage. He once again offered the party three riddles to solve in exchange for a reward. When the party solved all three of his puzzles, the bird sage teleported their ship closer to their destination; Cutting their trip in half. Although they were thankful for the reward, some on the ship viewed it as another bad omen on the high seas.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">On their third day at sea, the ship was attacked by another band of pirates. Cannons and splintered wood flew all over head as the party boarded the enemy ship. After taking some hits of their own, Hack’s cursed sword forced him to undergo a monstrous transformation. Unable to stop himself, Hack began attacking anyone around him, forcing the party to take him down. After a long, drawn-out conflict, the party stood victorious over the body of their fellow partymember. They were about to chuck the sword into the ocean when Crowley decided to retrieve it instead. He and his second-in-command argued over what to do with it, and once again the party noticed the growing tension between Crowley and the rest of the crew. Once the crew were finally following orders, the captain asked to speak with the party in his quarters.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Crowley was worried that his crew were planning a mutiny due to his decision of keeping the sword on board. He went on to explain to them that the sword once belonged to Captain Bendilin of the Broken Bridge Pirates; A notorious pirate group from back in the day. His actions were spurned by the pirate community because of the heat that came down on the rest of them. After this captain’s disappearance, a group of the most influential pirates groups came together to form The Five Shanties. They enacted a code within the pirate community to avoid further disruption of their business, and placed a bounty on the cursed sword so as to avoid a repeat of Captain Bendilin’s plundering. Crowley wants to bring it to them and asks The Boys if they would be willing to travel with them a little longer to San Cortina as a security measure against his scheming second-in-command. In exchange for this protection, they would receive a cut of the delivery job and most of the sword’s bounty whenever they meet in the future. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party decided to think on it while they secretly met with the first mate down below deck. The party met with the crew below as they were actively planning their revolt. After some attempts at poisoning the first mate, the party came down to brass tax with the shifting allegiance; In exchange for letting them usurp Captain Crowley, they would give them the sword and drop them off at the X on their map from the Druid metallic circlet. After much back and forth, as well as giving an opium addict the strength of a forest giant and sicking him on the first mate, The Boys decided to side with Captain Crowley. The next three days flew by, with a little ransacking going on, and they arrived at the coastal town of San Cortina where Crowley asked them for one last favor; To help him deliver the goods to the contact. As they venture into town to buy supplies and meet up with Crowley’s contact for the supply run, the party notices that there are a lot of Legionnaires accompanying the Night Guard patrols. It seems odd for such a small town, so Angus asked a local farmer about the going on. Apparently, due to Lord Gavyn’s high demand for annual crop yield, some of the farmers have been opposing the orders of their lord by hiding rations and attacking Pearl Legion food caravans. This group is known as the Scythborne, the group Crowley intends to sell the goods to. Timble and Crowley meet with their point of contact, a bar tender named Ivan, while Mordos meets with the mayor and Zellicek yells at passerby for information on Melville Barrett. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The group now awaits nightfall for their handoff with Ivan and the Scythborne. </p>
