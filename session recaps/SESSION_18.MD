<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 18</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">As the crowd began to disperse and discuss the revelations of the royal decree, The Boys noticed two groups of interest gathering in the plaza. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The first group they approached were causing a commotion at the palace doors. They were family members of palace servants demanding information on what has befallen their loved ones. Robert, one of the disgruntled peasants, told the party that he hadn’t heard back from his wife in over a month. A mysterious thread left alone for now.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The second group they approached were CROWN employees offering all registered bounty hunters a magical item to help them on their quests. The party argued over who would get to wear it, and eventually Mordos adorned the mystical bracelet known as MIRAGE; a ghostly apparition in the form of an eye peering through hexagonal tear in the fabric of reality. It will answer a single question a day to the party, one which can be answered if the information is available to CROWN’s Intelligence Agency. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After receiving this item, the group determined that their first priority in the next few days was to gain access to the coronation party. After chatting up some mercs messing with their own MIRAGE, the group found out that “people of importance” from Barronia and other cities were invited; One of which might be the fabled Gem of Barronia. They decided to look into Lady Myrathel’s RSVP status eventually, but before that they decided to tie up some loose ends around town.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">First they went to collect some reward money from the captain of the Night Guard for their efforts in proving Lady Myrathel’s innocence. Upon their arrival, Mordos and Hack noticed that a lot of the soldiers were eyeing them up and whispering suspiciously. When the captain came back with the party’s reward, he was accompanied by two aarakocra Legionnaires and night guard armed with net crossbows. He let everyone in the party go save for Veyron DeFalco who was taken into custody. Timble asked some of the guards there what the deal was and they told him that Veyron was wanted for holding up multiple people at knife point, most notably a Night Guard. Veyron was swiftly stripped of his possessions and jailed.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Unable and unwilling to help, The Boys made their way to the NBBA Offices to speak to the new GM, Isabella, about Edwin Florgersports’s will reading. After inquiring about the ownership of the Sea Shanties, Angus and Hack found out that Ulysses had cut himself into the ownership of this D3 franchise. Furious, the group made their way over to the Undertaker’s Guild to speak to the old man about getting their team back. When they arrived, they noticed that Ulysses was having trouble trying to exorcise a group of spirits within his quarry. They were making a racket whilst trying to dig into the ground; Their hands unable to interact with the stone below them. After seeing his efforts were in vain, Ulysses invited The Boys into the house.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before they were able to get a word in, Ulysses provided them with an official document signing away his share of the Sea Shanties;  All in return for the letter they had used to blackmail him in the first place. After a swift exchange, the group decided they had a few questions for the old undertaker. For starters, they inquired about the spirit conundrum they witnessed outside. Ulysses explained to them that they had been making a racket all week and that he had paid the town crier to see if any Spirit Samurai would be available to help. At first the party was unfamiliar with them, but when Ulysses explained that they were warriors from the base of Hell’s Mouth, they realized that Emiko fit that exact description; Spiritual samurai from the Tamashi Tribes that travel the lands in search of spirits to help move on to the afterlife. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party veered the conversation towards the Oni Clan in an attempt at gathering more info, and Ulysses showed a deep disdain for the occult group. He had been a part of the Oni Clan a long time ago, having worked alongside members like Bala Deepgift, Juunur, and Jebediah Dukes. The demonic priests, initiates, and assassins would work deep in the sewer system while businessmen like him would run the town. After he had passed his initiation and formed a pact with one of the archdemons from the Nine Hells, he used his late father’s business for the clan’s gain. He masqueraded as a reliable undertaker for decades, manipulating wills so that the Oni Clan could slowly gain influence and power in Barronia. However, after having done this for many years and now having a loving wife caught up in the mix, he decided to leave the Oni Clan. The only way out however, much to his dismay, was the life of his beloved wife. That was twenty years ago, and until his run in with The Boys he had not messed with any will readings. Before leaving Ulysses’ establishment, they made sure to ask him if any of the names on Zellicek’s list stood out to him; He recalled that Seldthorian Darkflight is the owner of The Dark Pour. They thanked him, paid for his kindness, and left his hut.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">As the group was leaving the quarry, Artie decided to help the spirits trapped there. She said a quick prayer and exorcized them; Telling the group that they wanted to “return to their bodies deep underground.” The group warned Artie to not do that in public again so that she won’t attract attention to her. After having spoken about the Spirit Samurai and seeing Artie’s powers, Mordos remembered to check the sending stones; Finding a message left by Emiko:</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">“Are they in town? Any news on Zaashu? Can’t come, busy with bounties. . . Tell Artie and others I say hi. . . Love you too, man. . .”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Meanwhile, at the Pearl Legion HQ, Veyron was waiting in a cell when he was introduced to Inspector Demarette; a detective with the Constabulary. He started badgering Veyron about The Boys, their whereabouts in the past month, and if they had a Druid with them. As he was about to question him over the slave caravan that was attacked on the road, the captain of the Night Guard, DelMonte, interrupted and dismissed the detective from the case. He grilled Veyron for a bit over his assault of one of the Night Guard officers, but the conversation quickly turned when Captain DelMonte revealed himself to be part of the Oni Clan. He gave Veyron an ultimatum; Return the magic lockbox they had found in the woods, or suffer the consequences for his illegal Druid friend. He agreed and was released under the condition that he keep the silver pentagram on him at all times. As they were leaving the Pearl Legion offices, Veyron was able to snag some files off of Inspector Demarette’s desk pertaining to The Boys.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Veyron rejoined the party and they all headed over to The Squire and Warden. Madame Baequiathem tried her best not to let them visit Lady Myrathel, but she eventually let them speak to her daughter. She greeted them warmly, and even though Mordos called the Lady of the House a “stone-cold bitch,” she was adamant that her mother was only doing what was best for her. The group cut straight to the chase and asked her about the coronation party; Lady Myrathel was invited and was allowed to bring two of The Boys as her security guards. The rest of the group still needed to figure out how to get in; Especially now that one of them was arrested by Captain DelMonte. They also let her look at Zellicek’s list and she recognized a few names; Bala Deepgift and Phoroxenor Markax are regular clients of hers, and Elliot Suarez is the captain of the Paladins of the Vulture the party had spoken to of pirate hunting.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Now realizing how big the Oni Clan’s web in the city is, they decided to use MIRAGE’s daily question to help gain some insight; WHAT DO YOU KNOW ABOUT THE ONI CLAN?</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em; font-weight: bold;">This group has been displaced many times, but the Oni Clan were original protectors of Hell’s Mouth. They worshiped the fiendish pantheon and would regularly descend the volcano to abduct mortal sacrifices for the archdevils that rule the Nine Hells. After centuries of this, they were eventually kicked out by the Wapoo Clan. After many failed attempts at retaking their ancestral land, they settled on the eastern island of Elustria and stayed there for a long time; Causing trouble to all the empires there and continuing their practice of sacrifice. They were eventually forced underground when King Barrett and his holy knights ran them off the island and across the bridges they had built. By the end of the Unifying War, this once mighty house had been extinguished. As of right now, there are no more existing members of the Oni Clan. Their relationships with all other Houses were extremely contentious, especially with the Wapoo Clan and the Mitsutake Nation. The one old house they had an amicable relationship with were the Imperiamotto Shogunate since they ruled over either side of the bridges. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">With all that they had heard about the Oni Clan in the past couple of days, the party decided to rest on it. The next morning they made their way up the northern coast to meet with the Whale Sharks and the Paladins of the Vulture.</p>
