<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 14 & 15</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Last time we left our heroes, they had descended into the depths of the Barronian sewer system and found themselves face to face with a demon worshiper and his pets. Not only did the party get to meet Bubbly Bob, Mordos’ summon, but a group of mysterious, skull-masked goons who finished off the demon priest as he fled the party’s might. The Boys tried to communicate with them but they fled deeper into the sewers. Some of the party members wanted to follow and investigate more, but the stench was too much for them to push through.  
After looking around the stinky battlefield, they discovered that the bodies the fiends had been feasting on were in fact Emmo’s brother and his hunting party. Gilbert had gotten his employees to drag their bodies from his place and dump them here. They also noticed a big empty box with “Golden Ticket Sweets Shoppe” stamped on the sides.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Veyron and Timble split from the rest of the party to deliver the bad news. They arrived at Man Smithing Co. and gave Emmo Twoman the Akadi idols they had pulled from the bodies. He tearfully took them and paid the party for their work after they had told them who was responsible for the murder of his brother. As promised, Emmo began to divulge some lore that Timble had been desperately seeking. Timble’s last name was in fact Aluvon, one of the many families that made up the Ahanjoo Clan. The reason he had told him to not use his last name is because the rest of the Ahanjoo hate the Aluvon Clan. It turns out that during the Unifying War, King Barrett the Bloody’s campaign, the Ahanjoo Clan were standing their ground; That is until King Barrett struck a deal with the Aluvons. They would go under a different name and receive safe passage into Elustria if they relinquished control of their ancestral home in the mountains just south of Circos. Ever since then, the Ahanjoo Clan has disowned the Aluvons for their treachery. Although he had no information regarding his parents, Timble thanked him for shedding a little light on the mystery that is his past.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The rest of the party knocked on some doors near the sewer entrance that weren’t under construction. After asking some strange questions, an elderly woman pointed them to the location of the Sewer Maintenance Guild where her nephew worked. They quickly made their way over to meet with the nephew, Thorn, who divulged some information in exchange for gold and a quick glance at a porno mag. He told the party that the city is well aware of the craziness that happens down below. Two factions seem to be fighting within the sewers; The Oni Clan, an old house that seems to run this city, and the Humming Oath, a mystery faction that the Pearl Legion is after. They also manage to buy some sewer masks off of Thorn so that they may later investigate more of the sewers without gagging from the stench.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party then all met up at The Squire and Warden to follow up on the missing women when they noticed that the building had been seized by the Night Guard. After questioning some of the guards coming in and out with boxes, they found out that Ian, the talent acquisition manager, found evidence that Lady Myrathel might be involved with the missing women after finding human remains in her room. In the midst of the chaos, the party managed to snag an open letter from Ian detailing something strange going on between him and Lady Myrathel. Unconvinced that this mystery had been resolved, The Boys set out into the night to follow the call girls. They split up and followed Yuki and Taro towards the south of town. While one group ended up terrorizing one working man’s night off, the other found Lady Myrathel meeting up with Yuki near the edge of town. She casted a powerful charm spell on her and had her walk into a nearby forest. The group reconvened and followed Zellicek’s tracking magic to a mysterious hut in the middle of an out of place swamp. The group slowly approached and noticed a few call girls tied up alongside two witches of the swamp. They noticed the party’s approach and began to fight back. </p>
