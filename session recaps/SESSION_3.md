<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 3</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party discussed staying together as the bounty hunters around them began sprinting out the door in search of Melville Barrett. After looking over the bulletin board, the group recognized Olburr Sootfoot as the leader of the highwaymen they saw on their way into Circos the day prior. The group bought rations in bulk and set out in search for their first group quarry.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">On their first day of travel, a mysterious, blindfolded man appeared from out of the woods with a corpse slung across his shoulder. He demanded many answers from the group before introducing himself as Zellicek Krall. He uses a seeing-eye mouse named Pooter who resides in his eye sockets. After the group lovingly nicknamed him Big Z, they set out together in search for Olburr; A first of many quests in their hunt for Melville Barrett. By the end of the day, the party came across the remains of the camp they had passed on the way from Drachenburg. After a successful Survival/Nature Check, the party was on Olburr’s heels.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The next day, the party managed to get the jump on Olburr’s crew and crushed them. They were no match for The Porno Boys and they even captured Olburr alive. The party looted some coin off of them, as well as a map leading to a pirate ship wreck. After making some false promises, he spilled the beans in an attempt to be freed. Olburr hails from slums of Avilon where he worked for a man named Draven Fireforge at the Frostholm Keep Smithing Co. The city’s underbelly is known for crafting and trafficking low to middle grade magical items; A byproduct of Avilon’s high concentration of magic universities and apprenticeships. They also found the reason he was wanted in the first place: a bag of holding with a mysterious magical stone in it. The glyphs are cracked and glowing blue. Unable to identify it, the party is deciding to find someone who will be able to tell them what it is, and maybe why the Librarians are after it. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">On their way back to Circos to collect on their bounty, the party encountered a group of royal guards destroying an ancient shrine of worship; Holding back a group of distraught villagers that were presumably there praying to Cersia, Goddess of Harvest. Here the Porno Boys learned that Lord Einar of Avilon was able to pass legislation that would enable the authorities to prosecute those who believed in deities other than the ones sanctioned by the state. The villagers would have been tried and the children would have been sent to reeducation camps if it weren’t for Angus’s divine meddling. He used Minor Illusion to create a terrifying version of Cersia that scared the guards away. The village elder thanked them with 200 silver and a promise to never forget what they had done for them. </p>
