<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 12</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The team spends another night at The Grappling Ivy Inn with a lot on their mind. The next morning Mordos sends another message to Emiko imploring for some more information on the whereabouts of this druid coven, and they split off to follow up on some interesting side quests.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Mordos, Timble, and Angus head over to Conrad’s Caravan Credit to begin investigating on behalf of Eddie Zolxarx. After much discussion on whether or not to set up the business for failure, they speak to a guard for hire named Tanaka. He is paid by Conrad to keep watch outside of his home across the street, and after selling him on the sleeping properties of a hallucinogenic pedal he tells the party that Conrad keeps interesting company. He is only ever allowed to let in a mysterious, hooded figure from time to time. Sometimes the hooded figure will bring a few guests along with her, some of which bear a striking resemblance to Artie; Blotchy skin, and red or silver hair. The group concludes that Conrad is involved with trafficking Druids secretly, and are torn on how to continue with the job given to them. They decide to set an appointment with the main man later that day and head out. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Semaj and Zellicek head over to The Squire and Warden to follow up on a job inquiry that Lady Mirathel had posted. When they arrive they are greeted by the irate talent acquisition manager of the establishment, Ian. He seems hesitant to let them in, but ultimately doesn’t have a choice but to let them see the Gem of Barronia. When they enter the room, they notice that it is much more lavish than the ones Veyron and Mordos had found themselves in a couple of nights ago. She has fake greenery hanging from the ceiling, a round bed surrounded by a hot tub lazy river, and more of the animated statues from before. After some haggling and whispered flirting, Lady Mirathel tells the group that a lot of the girls that have gone out on house calls have been going missing; Including her best friend, Annalyth. She wants them to find out what is happening to them and to report back to her, not the authorities.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">She has little information but tells them to check in with Ian; That she demands that he help them out with the investigation. She implies that Ian doesn’t seem to care about the missing girls and that he is refusing to get rid of house calls until it is safer; Says that he doesn’t like how much more say she has gotten over the years since she became popular. When the boys go to speak to Ian again, he gives them the information they ask for; A list of the women that have gone missing along with the names of those heading out for house calls soon. When prompted about it, he seems annoyed that he has to do any of this. Ian refuses to cancel house calls because the business would fail, and implies that a lot of the women that have gone missing had been yelled at by Mirathel in the past for not being proper courtesans. They leave the establishment, and when they meet back up with the group they notice that a lot of the women went missing in the south of town.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before heading back over to Conrad’s for their meeting, Mordos goes into The Curved Arrow Shoppe to buy some supplies. There he meets the owner, Trifflan Stoutbrewer, who offers him a free item if he uses his purchase when hunting a bounty to use it as promotion for his wares. After taking a look at the expensive stock, Mordos asks Trifflan if he had ever seen Emmo’s brother, Elron, around. The owner remembers seeing him twice; Once before the hunt, and after they had successfully hunted down the wyvern. Elron had briefly told him that they were looking to sell some of the creature’s parts to wholesalers operating close to Tentvilla.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Boys finally arrive at Conrad’s for their afternoon meeting. They bring Artie along with them, and after a little back and forth about how his credit service works they ask her to transform right in front of him. There is a little panic and a little misunderstanding, but the group manages to convince Conrad, real name Kriggle Longjohn, that they are not there to oust him. The group learns that Kriggle is working with a secret contact in the Silverhoods and Sakura, his mysterious hooded guest, to smuggle Druid families to safe havens around the country. The group asks if they may be able to meet Sakura to get Artie to one of these safe havens. Kriggle agrees once she comes back but tells them that it might take some time. He had received a concerning letter a week ago on her whereabouts:</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After reading the letter, the party learns that Eddie Zolxarx isn’t the only person looking to take advantage of Kriggle’s lucrative business. A man by the name of Bala Deepgift, owner of a casino called The Merchant’s Lust, has been hounding Kriggle to sell his business to him. He has been pushing off the meetings over and over again because he’s aware that Bala is a high ranking member of the Oni Clan; One of the Old Houses and the biggest crime syndicate in the country. He has heard that they get what they want by any means necessary; Especially through their many demonic pacts. He is struggling to find a way out of this situation, and the party offers to keep Eddie out of his hair as long as he can play along for now. Kriggle lets the party know that he will contact them via job board when Sakura returns.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After leaving Kriggle’s office, the group decides to wait for nightfall before heading over to Mr. Florgersports’ manor to look at his will.</p>
