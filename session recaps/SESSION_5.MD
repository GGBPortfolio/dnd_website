<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 5</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After defeating the royal guards with slaves in tow, the party looted the bodies and spoke to the now free prisoners. They found a letter on the captain’s body that alluded to a place called Magothica where the next few caravans were to be taken to for scientists in need of “helpers.” They also spoke to a tabaxi criminal named Zephyr. He spoke to them about the arms-smuggling route from Avilon to Styx, his former employer turned enemy Kaiser Lobo, his time spent in a lumber yard-prison camp up in Brosiel Forest, and a secret congregation of merchants taking place on one of the bridges of the Connecting Isles. The party asked him to inspect the mysterious stone they had pocketed off of Olburr in an attempt to learn something about it. He made note of the strange stone it was made out of, and ascertained that the runes are dwarvish. Semaj might know a thing or two about the dwarves since Zephyr had told the party that they used to live near Shilu Peak. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party released the other prisoners after hearing their story of worshiping “illegal” deities, and learned of the three branches of the military: the Pearl Legion, the Navy, and the Night Guard. They left the next morning, but the third child had not. Zellicek and Timble tried their best to ascertain any information, only getting her name, Artie, and seeing her ability to shapeshift into animals. The group hesitantly took her under their wing and moved on.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">While they were taking a rest on the side of the road, a friendly goblin by the name of Ho-Sei approached them with a promise of fantastical wears. However, before he was able to convince them, the high pitch of his voice awoke something deep inside of Semaj and Hack, making them want to kill Ho-Sei. After a few swings and heals and arguments, the little goblin touched the amulet on his necklace and vanished. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The next day was pretty uneventful, that is until the party went to bed. While on night duty, Semaj and Timble saw a group of spirits holding festival instruments appear from the tree line and surrounded them. Angus and Mordos noticed that the spirits responded to them participating in their festivities. Once the whole party had joined in, the spirits transformed into their old selves, and a giant apparition of the same spiritual glow appeared from above the tree line. It was a giant deer with a samurai riding on it. It swung its giant arm down to the ground, picked up the now-appeased spirits, and left the way it came.  </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The next day they were ambushed by a group of ninjas with very bad aim. Maybe that is why Mordos did not warn the party of them. However after realizing that Semaj was not the earth genasi they were looking for, Aric Tsukinomoto, the leader of the ninjas, Onyx of Solaris, called off the attack and they slinked away into the forest. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Finally the group arrived at the town of Flammenfurt where something seems to have gone down. The people are shifty and cautious, their storefronts and houses are war-torn and looted, and all means of transportation have been destroyed. The party made its way to a sort of funeral pyre on the edge of town where they met the mayor of Flammenfurt. He told the group that a band of bounty hunters named The Plaguebringers had wreaked havoc in search for answers to something most of the town knew nothing about; A rocky area near the coast where many ships have been beached due to terrible weather. Eventually The Plaguebringers left with one of the town’s children, Gurt, who presumably knew the way there. In return for free lodging, the party offered to try their best to rescue Gurt. The mayor also told them of another bounty hunter who was on the Plaguebringers’ trail named Emiko.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The group spent the night there and headed off into the wilderness towards possible treasure the next morning. As they made their way carefully through the magical woods, they stumbled upon an old battlefield. There amongst the overgrown horrors of war was Emiko praying at a shrine. When she drew her blade to confront Mordos, a figure closely resembling the giant samurai ghost appeared over her, almost as if he was her puppet master. After tensions were tamed, the party learned very little about The Spirit Daimyo; three rulers of spirits that wander the material plane. Emiko confirmed with the group that the daimyo that they had seen the other night was Shugo, Leader of the Restless. The samurai was not believing what they had told her until she noticed Artie hiding behind Zellicek’s leg; Insinuating that the only people that ever see the Spirit Daimyo are those who are special. After a little more prodding from the group, Emiko divulged that she was hunting The Plaguebringers to find their leader, Zaashu, for some mysterious reason (probably revenge). And thus The Boys added yet another member to their crew as they made their way to the sunken pirate’s treasure. </p>
