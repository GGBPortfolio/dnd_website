<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 9</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party arrived at their first toll house around evening where they dispensed their weapons for the night. Hack and Zellicek managed to sneak some in without alerting the Enya Clan guards. Artie was a little shaken up by them due to her recent past. After doing a quick perimeter check, the boys settled down at the bar for the evening where they met two strange travelers. The first one was a traveling elf bard by the name of Slack Jack Gerky. He asked the group for any stories that might serve him well during his performances. He paid the group for their story about the dancing spirits and meeting Daimyo Shogu. He also bought drugs off the group and told them that some of his friends at the Lunar Tea Shop Theater might be interested as well; Especially the famous playwright, Mr. Queen. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The other eccentric traveler was an old human named Captain Blackwood out searching for “old friends” of his. He claims to be a former merchant with knowledge of crossing the Connecting Isles without having to pay the expensive bridge pass. The captain advises the party to look into it with some of the local gangs. He also explained to the group that he once had a run in with the Crimson Vipers. Whether it was an amicable meeting or not, who’s to say. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After a peaceful night’s rest, The Boys woke up the next morning to a gruesome murder. The Butterfly Killer has struck again. After doing some investigating, the party discovered the identity of the victim, Edwin Florgersports, and that he is somehow involved in the National Boggle Ball Association in Barronia. As they look over the crime scene, Hack’s cursed blade asks him for a sip of the blood; Almost cleaning the scene of its unsightly contents. After having narrowed down the list of possible suspects and grilling the guards, they head back out on the road.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party picked up the pace to make it Barronia in time for the royal decree, using some high quality rations in the process. On their first day back out on the road, the group came across the body of a local flute player that had been mugged by bandits. After a little encouragement, Artie prayed and played the flute to commune with the woman’s spirit. She asked The Boys to take her to her village, Mizu no Sato, so that she may finally rest. The old woman’s family thanked The Boys, who claimed to be “spirit talkers,” and gave them rations and gratitude. After speaking to the village leader, the group found out that Zaashu, the man Emiko is hunting, had been through there a couple months prior.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">That night, the party found themselves surrounded by six fiendish beings. When Zellicek and Timble used their powers to detect them, they noticed that Mordos also had that same fiendish presence as the possible assailants. After demanding that the strangers come forward, Zellicek recognized the monk robes that they wore. Only the robes though. His amnesia from the trauma has affected him more than anticipated. They are the Hellion’s Bane, a group of devil hunting monks that travel Elustria to protect it from fiends that spawn around the world. They are related to the Wapoo Clan and seem to gain their powers through a mysterious ritual related to an archdemon of the Nine Hells. However, these younger monks don’t seem to know the full story of their lineage or practices. They do however recognize Zellicek’s scars as the M.O. of a hunter aptly named The Eye Collector. Someone who seems to have it out for members of the Hellion’s Bane keeps stealing their eyes. They were not of much help in identifying this man, however they believe that their former high priest Hiroshi Bunzo would. They said they split up with the main sect back around Palomeras, and that he may be able to enlighten Zellicek on the ritual and Eye Collector’s whereabouts. Before they parted ways the next morning, their leader, Masato, told them that they were heading to Avilon in hopes of starting their own guild; A devil-hunting squad not bogged down by the Wapoo’s traditions.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After traveling down the royal highway for a few hours, The Boys noticed two young boys carving something into the side of a tree. After a quick chase, the party captured the two boys and asked them about what they were doing out here. The bark had been peeled off the tree and was being used to write cryptic messages; To whom, they do not know. All they were able to ascertain from the two scared teens was that they were being paid to write these messages by a waiter at The Sundering Leopard. The most recent message that they had been carving into the tree read:</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">BACHELOR / DUSK / 4 DAYS</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party let the children go after trying to shake them down for some gold. Yet another loose end the group would look into at the capital of Elustria. After a few more hours of trudging along, The Boys noticed a huge, Edo Japanese castle pierce the skies in the distance. Once they broke through the last of the trees, The Boys had finally arrived at the walled exterior of Barronia.  </p>
