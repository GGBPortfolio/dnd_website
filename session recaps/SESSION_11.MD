<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 11</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Boys are escorted out of the theater after their evening was interrupted by the fabled intelligence agents known as The Librarians. Some of the group's members tried to stay and find out what this was all about. They heard a few patrons here and there speak about how “interesting” and “out of the ordinary” this rendition of the Mad Druid King’s story was. Mordos spoke to an elderly couple who were not fans of the play because it made the Druids seem “more human” than they really are. They also managed to speak to one of the lower ranked Librarians about why they were here. Apparently, Mr. Queen, the playwright behind this and many other shows, had been paid by The Librarians to write a “truthful” and daming rendition of The Rampaging Forest Campaign. However, after witnessing the show on opening night, they decided to pull the plug on the whole thing and are actively looking for Mr. Queen. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After failing to get a group discussion going at the local bar, the party heads over to the CROWN Offices to find lodging. Before being assigned to a particular inn, they recognized some things on the Barronia job board.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">They recognized a few names wanted for high bounties. Crowley Thorn, captain of the Red Raven Pirates and Hack Sparrow’s childhood friend. Kaiser Lobo, a warband leader that has been wreaking havoc south of Horse’s Neck. And finally, Prysm Talbot, a name the party recognizes through the letter they delivered to Mike Ontact. On top of the tempting bounties, they noticed three HELP WANTED posters from parties they also recognized: The Humming Oath, Emmo Twoman, and someone from the brothel looking for a private eye. After some discussion on what leads to follow, the group headed over to their assigned lodging, The Grappling Ivy Inn.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before they retired for the evening, going by the Doctors Within Borders, a few of the party members found Captain Blackwood at the bar, one of the suspects in the Butterfly Killer murders. He says that he knows nothing about the murder and is prodded no further. Instead they ask about what he is looking for in the city; An old friend of his, Ceraphina, used to own a store here but she seems to have moved on. He plans to move west to Palomeras to look for more of his old friends. They also asked him about the Melville Barrett bounty. He didn’t know much about it, however he had heard of a bounty related to Barrett from long ago. During the early days of the Elustrian Empire, someone by the name of R. Barrett was wanted for a lot of gold. One of the rumors was that King Barrett the Bloody wanted all of those related to him eliminated so that they wouldn’t be a threat to his succession. The bounty was taken down after years and no one ever claimed it. After that, the party hit the hay after their first, long day in Barronia.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The next morning, the group decides to go speak to the legendary blacksmith, Emmo Twoman, about his job listing. They meet the big, Santa Claus-looking fellow at his shop, Man Smithing Co, amongst the tall buildings of the inner city. Emmo’s brother, Elronn, and his hunting party have gone missing. They had sent him a letter about a week ago claiming to have hunted a wyvern south of Barronia. They were going to sell off most of the carcass to wholesalers in town then come by to his shop to drop off the good parts. However, Emmo believes that something must have happened to them because of how much time has passed. The party asks him if anyone else has inquired about his brother and discovers that people have it out for those of Emmo’s heritage; One that he surprisingly shares with Timble Von Trimble.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">They are both from the Ahanjoo Clan, one of the Old Houses. They worship the same deity, Akadi, Goddess of the Winds, and Emmo warns Timble Von Trimble to hide his necklace now that The Librarians are cutting down on those of other faiths. When Timble inquires further about what their family connection might be, Emmo tells him to never use his last name around any of the Ahanjoo Clan; Especially his brother who is a little hot-headed. He tells them that the Von Timble name is one with a checkered past amongst the Ahanjoo. Emmo himself doesn’t care much about that history and even hides his heritage because the people of Barronia do not like the Ahanjoo Clan for some reason. He promises to tell Timble more after they finish finding out what might have happened to his younger brother. They part ways having agreed on an amount of gold and possibly an Emmo Twoman Masterclass.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">While the rest of the party goes off to do their own thing, Angus, Mordos, and Timble go to meet up with Eddie Zolxarx at the Tipsy Troll Inn. His men spread out around the room while Eddie, a purple tiefling in brown leather armor, sits at a table across from them. He greets and grills them, but the party manages to get on his good side. He gives the party two different side quests:</p>
<p>-The leader of a caravan insurance agency called Conrad’s Caravan Credit is becoming a big name among the trading community in Barronia. Prysm wants to offer him “protection services” but he needs some convincing. Eddie says they might be able to twist his arm with information or set the stage for robbery that pushes Conrad to reconsider the initial offer. He tells the party NO SPECTACLES ALLOWED!!!</p>
<p>-Eddie forgot his anniversary and his girlfriend is not happy. He needs someone to sneak into her place during the day and read her diary. He needs something to be able to make it up for her.</p>

<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before heading back out to explore Barronia on their second day, they ask Eddie why Prysm Talbot, the leader of the Electric Coyote Band, has his own wanted poster. Apparently, while he was still doing street jobs himself, he had an unfortunate run in with the Paladins of the Vulture, killing two of them in a shakedown gone wrong. The Paladins of the Vulture are a guild in Barronia that specialize in protecting business fronts; A deterrent to would be thieves when they see their sigil on storefront windows. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The day is young, and the party has much on their plate. </p>
