<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 17</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Boys wake up the next morning and discuss what to do as they head over to the CROWN offices before splitting up. On their way there they hear a town crier deliver some news, much of which pertains to The Boys and their many leads. Once they arrive at the job board, a few things stand out to them. Angus and Timble notice a couple of posters related to the job they had received from Eddie Zolxarx; Help wanted signs from both the pirate hunters and the Paladins of the Vulture. Zellicek takes a couple down to investigate further; A Humming Oath poster and a bounty for a mysterious person named Jebediah Dukes. Mordos grabs a hold of the city ordinance poster requesting for help cleaning up the sewers. After having spent some time looking over the board, the party split off into two groups.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Zellicek, Mordos, and Hack head over to The Squire and Warden to follow up with Lady Myrathel and bring Ian to justice. After roping in the lady of the house, Madame Baequiathem, for some help, they sneak into Lady Myrathel’s room where the captain of the Night Guard is there; Consoling the distraught Gem of Barronia. The party told them what they had found in the swamp and revealed Ian’s intentions through the letter they snagged. Before Ian had a chance to defend himself, the captain took him away in cuffs. As he was taken away, Hack briefly heard Ian whisper something to him:</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em; font-weight: bold;">“I don’t know what you want, I did what YOU asked!”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Suspecting that something was awry, the group began questioning Lady Myrathel and Madame Baequiathem. They asked them what they knew about the Oni Clan. Madame Baequiathem claimed to not know anything about them. Myrathel told them that she was under the assumption that this old house was a thing of the past, but that she had heard a couple of her clients speak about it; Professor Yebcham in particular, especially when he is drunk.  She thanked them and gave the group their reward money for discovering what Ian and the hags were up to. They left the brothel, unsure of their involvement with the Oni Clan, but not before having Pooter listen in on Madame Baequiathem scolding Myrathel for divulging information about her client; Assuring her daughter that she would’t have gone to jail. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Meanwhile Timble, Angus, and Veyron left for The Dark Pour, a dingy bungalow turned bar on the northern outskirts of town. There they met an old, peg-legged man by the name of Trevor Darkmyth; Their point of contact from the pirate hunter poster they had found. He works for The Circle of the Whale, a sea-faring defense contractor similar to the Silverhoods, and runs a group on the side called the Whale Sharks; a group of fellow employees that don’t wait to be attacked by pirates but go after them as they rest along their shores. Trevor tells them that they know where the Red Raven Pirates will be hiding before they deliver their next shipment to the Electric Coyote Band. The group offer up their services alongside the 15 strong soldiers Trevor is providing for the attack. Timble also offers the pirate hunter to see if the Paladins of the Vulture will help. Trevor agrees and tells them that they might be more willing to join if they know they are hurting their competitors, the Electric Coyote Band.  They thank Trevor after buying him a drink and head out.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party met back up to discuss what had happened so far. After some quick shopping, they split off once more. Mordos followed up on one of the leads he had heard from the town crier at the CROWN offices. There he pretended to be part of the Crimson Vipers coming in to speak with the clerk. There he found out that they were looking to speak with the new leader of the Crimson Vipers and the Bladebone Brotherhood, Zaashu, about terminating their contract with CROWN. The clerk disclosed to Mordos that CROWN was trying to match an offer made by Full Belly Incorporated, a mysterious, private bounty hunting contractor. They seem to be offering a monthly stipend on top of bounty rewards. After he was done there, Mordos left Emiko a message:</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">“Sup Emiko! Crimson Vipers terminated CROWN contract, negotiating a new one. What’s up with you? I love you. . . Come to Barronia. Artie is safe. . . I love you. . .”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Zellicek made his way to the Royal Barronian University to look for Professor Yebcham, one of Myrathel’s clients. After locating his office, Zellicek pretended to be interested in doing more than just studying the occult under Yebcham. After outright asking to join the Oni Clan and getting shut down, the conversation took a violent turn. Zellicek asked the professor with a knife to his neck where he might be able to join the Oni Clan after seeing his pentagram necklace. He left the office with a location, The Glistening Fairy Bath Houses, and two letters of recommendation.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The rest of the party followed up on getting help from the Paladins of the Vulture to “help take down” the Red Ravens. After impressing their leader, Captain Suarez, with witty banter and push ups, the group disclosed their plans to hunt down the pirates that supply The Electric Coyote Band. At the mere mention of Prysm Talbot’s group, Suarez was eager to recruit his men to the cause. After offering him a random percentage of the loot they would find, The Boys left his office with his assurance to follow up with Trevor Darkmyth on setting up the ambush. With the pieces all set in motion, all the group had left to do was show up for the party. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party came together once more to follow up on Professor Yebcham’s lead to join the Oni Clan. They arrived at The Glistening Fairy Bath Houses, a nice establishment who’s clean exterior hides the front well. Zellicek prodded the owner of the establishment, Mr. Zarch, to join the Oni Clan. Zellicek was swiftly ignored as he seemed to be more interested in Mordos. He offered Mordos and Zellicek a massage, and they were taken upstairs by Helga and Olga, two orcish masseuses. While their backs were getting ravaged by these muscular women, and Mordos was distracted by his partner’s body, Zellicek noticed that the women were both wearing silver anklets with pentagrams on them. After some prodding, Helga told them to be careful not to poke around too much. She told them that the anklets “keep them there” and that the boys should be careful not to mess around with the Oni Clan. After some more questioning, they discovered that the masseuses see people that work in the sewers, only rarely working with anyone of worth within the organization. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">While Zellicek and Mordos’s approach was more direct, Timble and Angus decided to check out the bath house for clues. They placed their things in the bag of holding while Angus pretended to break his record for holding his breath under water. He secretly went invisible and flew up to investigate the other floors. He noticed a difference between massage rooms, some fancier than others, but the thing that stood out the most was a mysterious iron door with a padlock on it. When he returned back to the water, he and Timble spoke to one of the patrons, a sewer maintenance worker named Julius. He told them that if they want to work with the “right people,” ask for Donald Flormp at the Sewer Maintenance Guild. After everyone was done with their spa day sessions, Zellicek offered Mr. Zarch the letter of recommendation from Professor Yebcham. He told Zellicek to come back to him in a couple of days, alone. Before they left the bathhouse, Zellicek also managed to sneak a peek at the customer ledger and copied down names written in red who visited in the past three months.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party decided to rest for the night, and spent the next day drinking while playing Cambio and President; Forgetting to claim the extra reward from the captain of the Night Guard. The following day, The Boys made their way to the royal plaza where a crowd had gathered to hear the royal decree. </p>
