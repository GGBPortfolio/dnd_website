<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 6</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">We last left The Boys making their way towards the rocky, shipwreck-filled cliffs north of Flammenfurt. The party camped for the night after meeting Emiko Fireforge and asked her about where she came from and what she is after. Although she was not open to discuss the details of her mission, Emiko did let the party know of the lord she serves: Daimyo Sengoku, ruler of all vengeful spirits that roam the material plane. Whatever her overarching goal is, she needs the leader of the Plaguebringers, Zaashu, alive. She was also adamant about The Boys figuring out what to do with Artie, and warning them that they may be targets with her at their side.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The next morning, the party along with Emiko snuck their way to the cliffside where they saw a group of men and giant trolls hauling goods off of a beached shipwreck. After some planning, The Boys jumped into action and surprised the Plaguebringers. With their coordination and Emiko’s fiery talents, they managed to dispose of the group of bounty hunters. The party searched encampment and came across some incredible loot. Along with the gold and magical items, the party retrieved the Broken Bridge Pirates’ ship log that included two names of interest (Juro Tantakhan and Frederik Briar), and a treasure map that might lead to more of Captain Bendilin’s riches. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After having done some torturing of her own, Emiko found out that Zaashu is now running with another crew called The Crimson Vipers; The second most successful bounty hunting group on the CROWN Charts. She doesn't know why, but she intends to find out. The party members took turns asking her what she knew about Elustria. She spoke of some of the Old Houses: the Enya Clan, the Farfalleon Shogunate, and the Tantakhan Family (ask party members for more details). </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">She also spoke of the Wapoo Clan, the old house that resides close to her hometown of Styx. She was told as a child that they live at the summit of Hell’s Mouth, defending the fabled entrance into the Nine Hells. Before the group decides to venture forth towards Barronia, Emiko spoke briefly about her uncle, Draven Fireforge, Olburr’s contact in Avilon, and how they may be able to find some decent weapons there. All they must do to confirm that they had met was to speak these words to him: “The lantern burns brightest at the foot of Hell’s Mouth.” They parted ways and headed north toward the town of Flügelspitze, leaving Emiko with Gurt and Mordos’ other sending stone. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">On their first day of travel through the wilderness, the party came across the fresh remnants of a battle between two mysterious factions. They were able to identify one of them as the Enya Clan based on the crest hidden under some of their armor. The only defining characteristics of the other group they could ascertain besides their dark cloaks and red armor was the silver pentagram ring that one of them carried. Upon retrieval, Veyron recognized this ring from the world he came from; A sign of deceit and false hope given to Veyron by the very god that betrayed him. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Also hidden amongst the slaughter was an Enya courrier’s body clutching a strange lockbox; Almost as if he had been trying to run away with it right before his swift death. Hack tried taking a crack at the strange lock mechanism when a dark, necrotic energy hurt him and Angus. With more mysterious items added to the bag of holding, the group pressed forward.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party camped for the night with Angus taking first watch when Veyron seemed to be having some kind of nightmare. A single feather on his back began to pulsate and change color when he woke up in a cold sweat; Looking into the dark tree line to see many pairs of demonic eyes staring back. </p>
