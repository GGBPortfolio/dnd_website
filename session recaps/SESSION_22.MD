<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 22</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">We last left The Boys in one of the many ballrooms of the Grand Barrett Palace. As they mingled with the guests celebrating, or cursing, the crowning of the new king, the waitstaff instigated a fight between two groups; The Oni Clan members and the Librarians. As the different spells flew across the magnificent hall, the group decided to escape out the window with the guests they came with. They found Prysm Talbot hiding underneath a table while Lady Myrathel and her mother were being cornered by waiters with knives. After Hack and Veyron escaped into the night sky with the three guests, Mordos and Timble stayed behind to find out a little more about the attack. After convincing one of the waiters that they were part of the attack, he disclosed to them that they were members of the Humming Oath; Here to attack the Oni Clan and Librarian officials. One of their targets, the elusive Bala Deepgift, was busy fighting when Mordos and Timble took him hostage to save him from the attack. They landed safely in the crowd below and Bala disappeared into the night after thanking them.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Once they landed safely, Hack and Veyron received a note from Prysm Talbot with Conrad’s location; As promised for completing the last two jobs. The party reconvened in front of the Grappling Ivy Inn where they interrogated Lady Myrathel and her mother. They didn’t get much out of them save some information about the Humming Oath. They used to be a branch of the Pearl Legion; Holy knights that specialize in hunting demons with the help of their deity’s magic. Once the war was over and other forms of magic were banned, they were forced into hiding.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Boys let the women go back to the brothel so that they could rescue Conrad. They arrived at the address, a tiny hut on the southern outskirts of town, where Conrad was; Alone, beaten, and tied to a chair with the party’s payment stashed underneath. He told them that they forced him to sign away his business in exchange for his life and that he wanted to travel with them to show them the way to the druid coven. He needs to speak to Sakura urgently. Fearing that the city would be shutting down soon due to the attack on the palace, The Boys fled Barronia during the night.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">They traveled south for a couple of days; Finding a strange, metallic map with druid symbols on it leading to a place of interest near San Apalet. They arrived at a caravan outpost where they inspected the strange wares offered and spoke to a few people of interest. The first man was an opium addict who Timble coerced into giving them some information about the outpost. He told them of the other two people that the party spoke to. Willem Trilbet, former captain of the Plaguebringers, now a sad drunk with crushed dreams. Mordos spoke to him and found out that Zaashu had passed by a couple of weeks ago, accompanied by The Bladebone Brotherhood, and bought his crew. Willem is broken up by this, and curses his team for abandoning him; Unaware that his crew is no more thanks in part to The Boys. Through this conversation they got a description of Zaashu: an old fire genasi in a nice suit heading to Barronia.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party then went to speak to the mayor, Aldora Strongjaw; a former Night Guard having retired due to her displeasure with the state of Elustria’s new laws. Tearing families and communities apart due to their beliefs didn’t sit well with her. Veyron and Timble spoke to her about the rumors of anti-Elustria flyers being hung up around her town. Aldora is nervous about everything happening in the region (Kaiser Lobo attacking outposts, the New Wyrmheart barracks getting attacked, bounty hunting groups wrestling in the streets), and the last thing she needs is the Pearl Legion cracking down on the outpost for treasonous acts. Growing tired of the conversation, the party intimidated her for information about the surrounding woodlands; Finding out that Sakura Moonblade had in fact been bringing people through the outpost and into the woods south of them. She offered them 50 gold each for more information on the flyers, and The Boys decided to rest on it for the night.</p>
