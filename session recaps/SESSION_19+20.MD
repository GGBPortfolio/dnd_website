<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 19 & 20</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party arrived at the site given to them by the Whalesharks only to hear that the battle had already commenced. They rushed over to the beach where the three factions were locked in combat, and joined the fray to rescue Crowley Thorn and the Red Ravens. It was a grueling battle, cannonballs and blood littered the sand, but they once again emerged victorious. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Once the dust had settled, Eddie and the Electric Coyote Band appeared to collect their shipment along with their boss, Prysm Talbot. After some negotiating with the remaining crewmembers of the Red Ravens, Prysm introduced himself to The Boys. Having heard mostly good things from Eddie, he offered the party more work and even membership into the gang if they decided to stay in Barronia. They declined the offer to stay but were interested in one last gig in exchange for entry into the Coronation Party as Prysm’s security detail. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before they parted ways, Prysm warned the party that they were being watched from the cliffs above. Timble was able to freeze this mysterious tail in place while Angus charmed him into giving up information. The mystery man was Igor, a sewer-dwelling assassin working for the Oni Clan. After chatting him up, they realized that he had been tasked by Junuur to keep tabs on Veyron; Even if it meant following him across the country. They read over the letter he had been given on his task, noticing that there are likely different factions within the Oni Clan. With the help of Angus’ magic they had Igor send a letter to his contact claiming that Veyron had died on the beach and that the lockbox was gone. Then Mordos “initiated” him into The Boys by burning him alive. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After dealing with Igor and learning a little more about the Oni Clan’s intentions, they returned to the beach to speak to Hack’s friend, Crowley Thorn; An aarakocra from the same village in Maplesplint Woods. He let them know that they would be leaving from Barronia shortly after the Coronation Party, and they have two locations to choose from as their destination. One is to St. Curtshol in the Connecting Isles where they were delivering goods to someone in the Noburo Clan. The other is to San Cortina, all the way across the Miolmorr Sea, to deliver weapons to a mysterious group called The Scythborn. When asked about his trade, Crowley seemed to imply that he could see a war brewing; Especially after King Barrett’s death. With old houses such as the Shilu Shogunate and the Wapoo Clan still not bending the knee to Elustria’s forces, he belives that conflict seems inevitable. After speaking to the group a little bit about his hometown’s relationship to the Farfalleon Shogunate, the secret markets on the bridges, and Hack’s cursed sword, Crown gave the group a sending stone to contact them when the time arrives.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The group headed back to Barronia, and as they arrived they heard a message from the town crier directed at them: “Conrad’s Caravan Credit requests that their clients, Doctors Within Borders, come speak with them concerning important paperwork.”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">They immediately went over to Conrad’s establishment to see if there was any news on Sakura and Artie’s transport to safety. However once they arrived they were met with a ton of new security placed by the Electric Coyote Band. Timble managed to embrace the scared secretary as an “old friend” who whispered to him that Conrad was in danger. He also managed to slip a note to Timble that read “Silverhoods HELP.” After some discussion outside Conrad’s business, Mordos drank his invisibility potion and snuck inside to report on the situation. He noticed that Conrad wasn’t in his office, but Eddie Zolxarx was. Mordos revealed himself and they chatted. Eddie disclosed that he had been keeping tabs on Conrad after The Boys had refused to tell him how they were able to coerce the business owner, and discovered the Druid trafficking scheme after the party had delivered the two prostitutes from the swamp hag’s hut. He offered Mordos a deal; One last gig in exchange for 200 gold a piece, the three security detail passes to the Coronation Party, and Conrad’s life. After accepting the terms, Mordos was given a list of addresses, and a copy of a sigil Eddie wants him to carve into the side of each building. Once back outside with the rest of the group, the party discovered that the sigil is a magical rune for Delayed Fireball.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before discussing what to do about this next gig, The Boys decided to do a makeover. They leaned into the Doctors Within Borders persona so as to evade any eyes on them; Whether they worked under or around the law. They came out of a local costume store in nurses outfits, and Veyron even went as far as dying his feathers red.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After their quick shopping spree, the party decided to investigate each of the addresses before marking the sigils. On their way there, they noticed that they were being tailed by one of the security guards they had seen at Conrad’s and decided to play the part. When they checked out the places on the list, all save one were warehouses with rag-tag street thugs acting as security. The last address on the list was a bar filled with working class patrons. The party was able to seduce one of the drunks into a dark alleyway where they forced him to disclose information. This bar and those warehouses all belong to the Petty Pistol Posse, a rival gang to the Electric Coyote Band. He let them know who they could speak to inside, and after chatting up the bartender they were able to meet the main man, Warbleman. The party took him away from his poker game to explain the situation while dancing around the truth. They struck a deal with the leader of the Posse; In exchange for some of their opium they would disclose the locations set to go up in flames. Warbleman agreed and they parted ways. The party made sure to etch the first sigil onto the bar for Eddie’s tail to see.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before heading back to the Grappling Ivy Inn to rest, Angus and Veyron decided to look into the mysterious lockbox the Oni Clan wanted. They managed to find the owner of a magical armour shop, Dorian, who helped them open the box in exchange for a small discounted fee. After successfully opening it, Angus added a few new items to the bag of holding.</p>
<p>-Blueprints to a giant mansion named “Terminus Machina Manor”</p>
<p>-A list of names. One etched in red: Xandrek Vorrin</p>
<p>-A mysterious, three-pronged rotating key with the same emblem on the manor’s blueprints</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After trying to ascertain what any of this was, Dorian informed them that Terminus Machina Manor is a famous destination in the city of Avilon. It is Lord Einar the Clever’s home, and it is herald as the cutting-edge union of magic and machinery. MIRAGE disclosed to the group that due to these innovations, Avilon is seen as “the city of the future.”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">While this was going on, Mordos paid a quick visit to the bathhouse where Mr. Zarch definitely didn’t recognize him. He was able to take a peek at the customer ledger and noticed that Junurr had been in that day. He left contemplating whether Igor’s message had been received. After that the party rested and continued their gig for Eddie the next morning; All the while collecting their opium payment from their rivals.</p>
