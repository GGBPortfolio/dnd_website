<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 1 & 2</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">
A ragtag group of bounty hunters all end up at the First Impression Tavern in Drachenburg; Each hunting down their own quarries. Some fought each other, others collaborated, and one even summoned their trusty steed as the world’s friendliest barricade. In the end, each party member was able to collect their heads and call it a night. Angus and Hack kept theirs alive (off-screening him later) and were able to collect some valuable information. The druid sympathizer was quick to talk, giving away the location of a druid coven somewhere north of Circos. The next morning the party decided to stick together on the road on their way to the CROWN offices in Circos.</p> 
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before leaving Drachenburg, Timble decided to pay a visit to the local opium dealer and steal some of his supply. On the first night, the group encountered a strange tower that shined like the moon. It was barely visible during the day, only showing itself fully at night. The only entrance available was a single small door with a plaque that read: “Speak the secrets of the scholar to enter.” DeFalco tried his hands at the door. . . twice. The first time he was knocked back by strong magical lighting, nearly killing him. He died the second time. . . (and Angus healed him before a minute passed).</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">On the second day the party encountered a small caravan of farmers heading towards Circos. Their village elder, Terry Longnose (I’m so sorry, that was not on purpose I swear), asked the party for help seeing as one of their carts was broken. After much haggling and bullying, the party agreed to spend the night and travel with Terry’s group in exchange for a cart once they arrived. On the third day, the party came across a suspicious group lying in wait on the road ahead. After some investigating, planning, and reckless endangerment of a child, the party was able to slip by no problem after Semaj Yak scared the shit out of the group’s leader (the one who’s WANTED poster they’ll see very soon).</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">On the fourth day, the party arrived at the city of Circos, home of CROWN HQ and former military stronghold of King Barrett the Bloody during the Unifying War. After saying their “goodbyes” to Terry, they head off to collect their bounties. Once inside the CROWN HQ, the party witnessed the announcement of CROWN’s biggest bounty: Ten million platinum for someone named Melville Barrett. No more information was shared about this quarry except that the King would be delivering a royal decree a month from now.</p>
