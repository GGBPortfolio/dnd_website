<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 13</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">As night fell on Barronia, The Boys made their way to Edwin Florgersports’ pent house within the city walls. Zellicek used Pooter to survey the building's surroundings and came across another sewer grating; This time noticing torches and the distant voices of sewer dwellers. After much discussion, Hack decided to take Pooter and drop him into the estate via sun roof while Mordos created a diversion at the front door. Pooter was able to locate Edwin’s office and secret safe while also finding two letters of interest; One from an angry sports enthusiast, and another to a secret lover.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Once Mordos had started talking Mrs. Florgersports’ ear off, disguised as the angry enthusiast, Hack entered from above and quickly opened the safe. Inside was the man’s will and testament, along with another two letters of interest. One pertaining to the shady dealings of his undead reading process with a man named Ulysses. The other about his past as a member of the Crystal Bulls; Someone named Primmen may have been after the Boggle Ball mogul over a “Junisira Grove job.”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before they were all able to flee the scene, Mordos was taken in by two Night Guards who were worried about the disguised 12 year-old. He managed to get a glimpse of the inside of the Pearl Legion’s headquarters and sneaked out disguised as one of them.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">They all regrouped before heading back to the inn and decided to pay this Ulysses fellow a visit. After asking around they found themselves at a strange site within the walls. A tiny, swampy quarry along the city walls with the rickety home of the Undertakers’ Guild at the bottom. As they went down to the house, pretending to have a dead Veyron to bury, they noticed many lost souls hanging around the house. They managed to get an audience with the old undertaker and threaten him with Edwin’s letter. Ulysses explains to them what his job entails, and The Boys pay him to lie about Hack and Angus owning the Sea Shanties. He agrees under the condition that they give him the letter once it is all done. All will go smoothly unless the law gets involved. Ulysses draws a hard line there due to his past of “shaping the town.”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The next morning, before the group heads out in different directions, Emiko responds to Mordos’ previous message: “Druids hide in forests since they are adept in nature. Also careful asking about the Caravan Coven. Heard any news on the Crimson Vipers yet?”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Timble, Mordos, Hack, and Semaj make their way to the Tipsy Troll Inn where Mike Ontact tells them where to next meet Eddie. They make their way to The Stunning Tailor Merchant in Textile Path where they notice some suspicious seamstress work going on. They make it to the back room where they meet with Eddie and his goons. Timble tries to finagle his way around telling Eddie about Conrad’s secret without paying him the protection fee they had collected. Eddie is not amused and even threatens violence once the argument gets heated. Timble reluctantly hands over the protection fee and is told that they will eventually have to turn over Conrad’s secret. After the dust settles, Eddie offers them another job in exchange for higher pay and possibly passage across the Connecting Isles. They have caught wind of the pirate hunters, The Whale Sharks, going after their suppliers, The Red Raven Pirates, and they want to use this opportunity to knock out two birds with one stone. They ask the party to make sure the Paladins of the Vulture also show up to aid the pirate hunters so that they may dispose of them both and use that as leverage against their suppliers. The group agrees and heads out to meet up with the others.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Zellicek and Veyron head over to Tentvilla to look into Elron’s disappearance. They stop by a random tent and end up barging into a very large, nice establishment with tables and a bar. They ask the bartender a few questions about Elron. After giving a vague description, he tells them that he and his group had been partying after selling off parts of a hunt they had been on. After doing a little investigating at the local open-air market, and robbing a nobleman at knife point, the two of them discover that a couple of lucky vendors were selling high priced wyvern parts they had gotten from Bluebelly Wholesale Goods. They followed up on this lead by speaking to employees of the wholesaler’s warehouse and found out that the owner, Gilbert, had an argument with Elron the day before he received the parts. The rest of the party joined up with Zellicek and Veyron once they started heading over to Gilbert’s apartament. Once they arrive at the worn-down building they speak to the cranky, old landlord. He tells them that Gilbert had been late on rent payments ever since Mr. Deepgift purchased the property, and recently had the nerve to throw a loud party. He believes that he saw someone of Elron’s description there but was surprised at how quiet it got all of a sudden. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The group went up stairs to Gilbert’s flat to find it in disarray. They see that there were a lot of footprints of different sizes, a poison punch bowl, and a little bit of a struggle dragging something out of the apartament. A neighbor of Gilbert’s, also held at knife point by Veyron, said that they saw some of Gilbert’s employees drop by with carts to take some big stuff away from the building. Thanks to Zellicek’s impressive tracking skills, they manage to follow the cart’s dirt trail to the outskirts of town where they see a giant well opening leading to the sewer system of the city. Timble and Zellicek go down to investigate while the rest wait above. Suddenly, Veyron’s cursed feather begins to burn once more as demonic eyes stare back at the two who decided to venture into the well.</p>
