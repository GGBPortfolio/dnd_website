<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 23</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Boys woke up the next morning at the caravan outpost, ready to make their way to the coven just west of them with Conrad. After some last minute shopping, they headed off into the woods.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">On the way there, the party exchanged notes and discussed what they had learned in the past few, hectic nights. After some back and forth, Timble discovered that Zashuu was in fact an alias for a man they saw at the coronation party; Eli Tannerman, a fire genasi running around Elustria recruiting bounty hunters to his outfit called Full Belly Inc. Mordos made sure to message Emiko about this new lead on her mark. She responded that at the moment she was “around Palomeras, tracking down the vice captain of the Crimson Vipers.”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">As Conrad led them through the woods towards the coven, the party saw smoke rise from the horizon. They took all day to arrive at the sight of a great battle between the Night Guard and refugee Druids; One of which being Sakura Moonblade in her triceratops form. After she had quickly dispatched the Night Guard, and threatened one of the Druids, the party made themselves known. Tensions were high, so there was little chatter as the party ventured the rest of the way to the coven with the Druid warriors. Once there, they were separated from Sakura and Conrad and told to go to the other camp; A place where non-druid refugees were allowed to stay. Strangely enough there they met with Baron Moses, the druid that Sakura had threatened the night before.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After offering him a bottle of liquor, Baron opened up to the group about his past. He was born an Enya retainer, working on his father’s farm until he joined the Elustrian Navy at 17. When he was 24 he accidentally discovered his Druid powers in front of his crewmates. He was swiftly arrested and held prisoner up north in Brosiel Forest. When asked about why it took him so long to discover his Druidic powers, Baron revealed to them that Druids don’t have to be born by those “touched by nature.” Druid children can be born to random couples which is one of the main reasons why so many are fearful of the Three Child Prophets. Artie began to shed tears, and shared with the party that she was in fact born from non-Druids who sold her into slavery.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After learning all of this information, the group deduced that Melville was in fact one of the child prophets of Druid legend; Similar to Artie, or so they suspect. The strange, infinite rooms that Hack had stumbled upon were used to forcefully breed a prophet under the crown’s watchful eye. The lords of the court are desperately looking for him, and allude to knowing who the third prophet is (not Artie). For what reason they need all three prophets still remains a mystery.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Baron went on to tell them that the Pearl Legion were in the middle of transferring him to some remote location south of Circos when Sakura saved him. He then spent 4 years in the coven, hoping that Sakura would become more proactive in their approach. While he was staying safely tucked away in the woods, they were visited by a mysterious Druid named Charlie Wilts. This man told them of King Jerosha, the real tale of the Mad Druid King, and how he was a prophet willing to stand up to Elustria. This led Baron to despise Sakura and the other Druids for their “pacifism.” He revealed to them that he and some of the other druids were the ones who attacked the re-education camp in New Wyrmheart a week ago. Sakura was upset at him earlier because they believed that the Night Guard followed to their location. Baron does not regret his actions and insists that Druids like Sakura could be doing more. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before The Boys were called upon by Conrad to see Sakura, Baron shared with them his plans for the future. While imprisoned in the logging camps of Brosiel Forest, he noticed a majestic, orange bird staring at him. He believes that this was a Druid, and that there might be another coven somewhere up there. He plans on taking his fellow outcasts with him to recruit more Druids to Charlie Wilts’ cause.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Boys were escorted to Sakura’s tent while the rest of the refugees packed their things. She thanked them for saving her husband, Conrad, from the Oni Clan and Prsym Talbot. Curious to learn all that they could, the group started off by asking about her contentious relationship with Baron. After he had been rescued from a chain gang and staying in the coven for so long, Sakura felt that he believed that they should go on the offensive; Recklessly leading the Night Guard to their safe haven. She blames an extremist group hiding within the Druid communities called The Jeroshans who call for all Druids to take a stand against Elustria. Sakura seemed pretty tense talking about King Jerosha’s actions and how they affected public opinion of Druids. She tells them that due to Baron’s stupidity they are now forced to relocate. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party introduced Sakura to Artie who was, not surprisingly, dumb founded by her abilities. After speaking to the child who had been with the group for so long, Sakura preemptively thanked them for bringing Artie to them. They were hesitant to give her up to strangers, which took Sakura and Conrad off guard. After much discussion, The Boys allowed them to take Artie under a few conditions. They had to know where she was going, she had to have access to the sending stone they gifted her for regular contact, and the possibility of future visitations. Sakura agreed all while nervously gripping the hilt of her katana. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Now that trust had been established, the party let her know that Melville Barrett is one of the prophets, and that the royal court knows who the third one is. It takes her a moment to process all of the information The Boys have sat on her lap. She goes on to tell them that one of their great fears as a community is the Druid Prophets falling into the wrong hands; Trained to be powerful weapons for their masters. The Druids knew that all three prophets were alive, ages 8, 13, and 21, and were in desperate need to find them. The party could tell from this explanation that Sakura is grateful to them for having not only brought them Artie but to have divulged this information. She lets them know that they are planning on taking Artie to Junisira Grove where they will test to see if she is in fact one of the prophets. After all was said and done, the party watched as the Druids left inside giant whales towards the horizon.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Before the other camp left, the group met with Emmo Twoman; The blacksmith on the run from Barronia’s Night Guard. He thanked the party for their help by gifting them an old Ahanjoo blacksmith’s guide to legendary weapons and materials. Once the camp left, The Boys called for their friend, Crowley Thorn, and struggled to find a way to communicate to them where they were.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Boys now wait along the coastline for their pirate friends to pick them up. </p>
