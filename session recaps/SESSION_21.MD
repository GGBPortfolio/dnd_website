<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 21</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The day of the coronation has finally arrived!!! The Boys cleaned up and bought clothes for the occasion while a couple of them ran some last minute errands.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Veyron DeFalco returned to the Pearl Legion HQ with the empty lockbox in hand. After a quick confrontation with Cpt. DelMonte, one of the Oni Clan’s inside men, Veyron received all of his equipment back along with his freedom.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Zellicek Krall looked over the documents they had received from successfully opening the lockbox, and found something familiar about the name Xandrek Vorrin; Its phonetic roots are very similar to his own. Suggesting that this mystery man might be connected to the Hellion’s Bane. Curious about what any of these contents mean, Zellicek made his way to the Royal Barronia University to gain access to their wealth of knowledge. Upon his arrival, he saw the Librarians still busy collecting and burning books, both from the public line spanning a few blocks and the university’s library. After convincing the Librarian officer guarding the library’s entrance, he was escorted to a recently curated section of the library where the “real history” can be found; All the while other officers were busy checking the rest of the literature for “falsehoods.” While he was given Lord Einar’s autobiography by the enthusiastic officer, Zellicek convinced Artie to make a mad dash for the Grappling Ivy Inn with one of the books being collected for burning. She was able to grab one and barely managed to escape as the other officers shot at her.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Now with the party all together, they made their way to the plaza in front of the palace where the festivities were being held. Amongst the many celebrating they found both Lady Myrathel and Prysm Talbot waiting for them by the palace’s entrance. Before heading in as their security, Eddie Zolxarx warned the party not to make a mess of things; It is very important that Mr. Talbot has a good night of rubbing elbows. This was what they needed to ensure in exchange for Conrad and gold. As the rest of the party went in as invited, Angus snuck in using his invisibility magic.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">They made their way into the main hall, a giant throne room with all kinds of people waiting in anticipation for the coronation. Angus noticed that there was some tension growing amongst the Librarians and some of the other guests; namely those hanging around Bala Deepgift and Lady Myrathel. After letting enough people in, the main doors closed and a procession of all the Old Houses. Each group came heavily guarded down the middle aisle with a bannerman holding their sigils aloft for all to see. The party noticed that some of the Old Houses were not present but their banners were still carried in ceremony. After them, the Lords of the Royal Court came down the line. First it was Lord Einar of Avilon, followed by three Librarian high officers and two hulking, mechanical monstrosities. Afterwards came Lord Brozzerious accompanied by many pretty courtesans. Finally, Lord Izyr helped Lord Zurvan down the aisle to thunderous applause. After asking Lady Myrathel and her mother, they claimed that the applause was due to Zurvan’s status; He is the oldest lord who helped start the Unifying War with King Barrett, and he runs CROWN. Strangely enough, they did not know much about Izyr, but the party recognized his sigil based on the letter they retrieved after rescuing Artie a month prior. After them, lone bannermen also held aloft sigils of the lords who did not attend that day. Once everyone was in, Lord Einar began his speech as a young man sat on the throne.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em; font-weight: bold;">“After much deliberation from the members of the royal court, I have the honor of introducing to you all our new ruler. May his wisdom take us to new heights, may his benevolence maintain our peaceful union, and may his youth grant us a vision our age has blinded us too. ALL HAIL EDUADIÑO FARFALLEON BARRET III! ALL HAIL OUR NEW RULER! LONG LIVE THE KING!!!”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After the cheering had settled and the important guests were able to briefly meet the new king, the party followed their guests into one of the many ballrooms of the palace where they began to mingle with the upper crust of Elustria. Much was discovered and discussed as the night went on.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Hack managed to sneak away from the ballroom and found himself in a strange room deep beneath the palace. As he heard voices approach, he quickly hid as the Lords of the Court who were present had an emergency meeting about the fate of Elustria. It is here where Hack discovered that Melville Barrett, along with two powerful companions, were heading towards Palomeras where Lord Gavyn was setting a trap. After much more was discussed, the lords went their separate ways. Before leaving the room, Hack investigated the strange crystal ball that was on the table. He was able to discern that this was a way for someone to be present at the table, and by the looks of it they were not happy with the lords. He tried his best to see if he could catch up to Lord Brozzerious’ ship, only to end up in a strange place higher up in the palace. What he came across might allude to the sinister plot behind the missing servants they had heard about shortly after the Royal Decree.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">When Hack rejoined the group, they decided to eavesdrop on a few of the conversations around the room. As the party was busy discussing what they had overheard, a loud insult cuts through the banter and music: DEMON FUCKERS!!! They notice that the Librarians and Oni Clan members are inching toward each other as members of the waitstaff seem to be instigating a fight; So much so that one of them fires a lighting bolt from the Librarians side. Hitting one of the Oni.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The room sparks into an all out magical brawl as The Boys’ guests hide from the damage.</p>
