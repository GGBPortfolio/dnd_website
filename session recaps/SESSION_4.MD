<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 4</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After a few days’ journey, The Boys arrived at the outskirts of Circos late one afternoon. There they decided to chat up Olburr Sootfoot one last time. The party learned more of the Librarians; They were looking for him after a botched home robbery ended in the death of a high ranking Librarian agent. He is unaware of what the mysterious stone is or does, and had only taken it because the Librarians were known for confiscating a lot of magical items. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The party persuaded him to try to use the stone, and the man burst into flames. Whatever it is, it seems broken. The Boys decided to forfeit the bounty on this one to avoid any trouble from this mysterious intelligence agency. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After some discussion, the party decided to look into the possible stash location Olburr had spoken of; A pirate shipwreck off the eastern Elustrian coast. They split up for the night to prepare for the coming journey. Mordos and Angus chatted up strangers to ask them about the surrounding lands and the Barrett bounty. Angus found a room for the night whilst Mordos decided to visit some farms outside of the city’s walls. Veyron and Hack purchased rations for the days ahead and also cornered Tarryon, owner of Tarryon’s Traveling Trinkets, demanding a map of the region. Semaj and Timble paid a visit to The Sturdy Shoulder, a shield and armor shoppe run by a middle-aged gnome named Samelith. After perusing his wares, Timble haggled with the gnome and wound up giving him opium in exchange for store credit. Out of curiosity, Semaj took a pull from Samelith’s favorite pipe and was zonked. They took to the streets to sleep and were promptly escorted out the city’s gates. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">While the rest of the party was sound asleep, Mordos visited one of the many pig farms that surround the city. There he managed to hunt and cook one up, right along the fence of the enclosure. However, he couldn’t fully savor the feast he had prepared because the owner came out to confront him. He was promptly roasted alive, sharing the same fate as his pig. Before the neighbors were able to get out to investigate, Mordos had vanished into the night.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Boys set off the next morning towards the pirate treasure’s location. On the first day, they encountered a traveling troupe of performers led by a half-orc named Lucifrax. He is out spreading the news of an amazing play being put on in a theater in Barronia. They received a flier:</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The Lunar Tea Shop presents “From Kids to Kings.” </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">They also learned of Lady Mirathel, a famous prostitute and performer nicknamed The Gem of Barronia, and Emmo Twoman, a world-renowned blacksmith. After a quick grope from Veyron DeFalco, they parted ways.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">On their second day, the group encountered a caravan composed of royal guards escorting a chain gang; Some criminals, some children. Timble tried buying the children to save them, but they were not for sale due to their significance in Druid culture. Angus remembered from his extensive travels that it might have something to do with the Three Child Prophets he had heard from fellow bards on his travels. After some planning and sneaking, The Boys managed to waste the guards with ease and rescue the entire chain gang.</p>
