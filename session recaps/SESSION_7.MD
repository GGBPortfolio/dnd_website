<p style="text-align: center; font-size: 24px; font-weight: bold;">SESSION 7</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After the group was abruptly awakened by demonic pursuers, they went into battle and emerged victorious. Once the demonic sulfur had been collected from their corpses, Veyron told the party that this had happened a few times in the past. He has been marked by Vanos, a deity from his own universe, and was sent here for unknown reasons. It is his mission to figure out why, and how to return home.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">The next morning, The Boys encountered a group of guards watching over some artificers hard at work. They were busy building what looked to be a lamp post. Upon further inquiry they discovered that this was part of an initiative from the government to make the Royal Highway safer. These are not ordinary lamp posts; Their magical glow is supposed to not only light the way for travelers but to deter magical beasts and fiends from entering within range. They spoke of a similar, much more powerful version of this technology being used around Brosiel Forest: the one said to house a prison camp.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">By the end of the day the party made it to the town of Flügelspitze. They arrived at the Enya-controlled town late in the evening so most of them made their way into the local inn. Veyron decided to synchronize and spotted a mysterious camp fire in the woods right outside town. When he flew over he was greeted by three hunters, and was offered food and fire side company. They spoke to him about some rumors they had heard of a wanted man by the name of Kaiser Lobo making his way through Horse’s Neck. The band of warriors is supposedly so fierce that they bullied their way through a local toll house without paying; And rumors tell of Kaiser having beaten an owlbear with his bare hands. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Meanwhile at the inn, the group splits up to eavesdrop on conversations happening in the mess hall. Angus hears a toll house worker speak of the gruesome murder of a strong guild leader and the butterfly blood paintings smeared across the walls. As Angus riffed in front of a group of impressionable pub patrons, the table next to him did not seem as amused by his Enya Clan comments. When inquiring about their worries, the armored Enya members were informed of the bloody mess The Boys had found a day's travel ago. The leader of the table, Kenji, offered the party room and board in exchange for information first thing in the morning after speaking to his superior. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Semaj and Zelliceck went over to speak to an old, tiny man scribbling away in his notebook. The man identified himself as Bullder Ross, an alchemist traveling in search of ingredients. He explained to the young adventurers that he was looking for an electric elderflower, a very important ingredient in making revivification potions. He was able to locate where they grow, a cliff side next to the ruins of an old town, however it was currently the camp of a big pirate group with a red bird painted on its sails. The party offered to help him in exchange for a revivification potion. They also found out that Bullder Ross was actually a fairy, just like Angus McCloud, who would hide his wings due to his fear of being persecuted. After some words of encouragement from Semaj, the old alchemist told them to meet him at his shop in Barronia called The Artisanal Rooster once they had acquired the flowers.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">After some discussion amongst themselves, the group decided to lie to the Enya captain, Kenji, when he asked them if they had retrieved anything from the battlefield. It seemed like they were supposed to receive the mysterious lockbox from another one of the old houses; the Oni Clan. Kenji offered the party 2000 Gold in exchange for the documents the group assumed were inside the mysterious box. After investigating the lockbox some more, the party asked Bullder if he recognized any of the glyphs found on the magical lock. The old alchemist told them that the neighborhood he lives in is littered with graffiti that resembles the markings. From what Bullder tells them it seems like Barronia has a major gang problem, especially on the outside of the walls where his shop is located. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">And with that, The Boys set out north in search of flowers as they make their way to Barronia. </p>
