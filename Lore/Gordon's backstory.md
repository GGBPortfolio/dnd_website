Veyron DeFalco lives happily with his family in the country of New BEUGHland. The Columbia Empire is dominant and Veyron serves as a well respected general who has led countless battles to victory against all who oppose the empire. (The Columbia Empire was the result of a revolt against tyrannical leaders, who fled and formed small countries around New BEUGHland) The deity of the land is Vanos, who assisted the Colombians with their revolt and with her blessings had assisted in victories against their foes. 

Suddenly in one battle, Veyrons forces are completely destroyed by unforeseen power from the tyrannical enemy, with evidence of Vanos’s influence. When Veyron prays to Vanos he finds out that she has betrayed Columbia as the tyrannical leaders have agreed to surrender the land to Vanos’s demons. Vanos only helped Columbia as a ploy to gain influence, in the end, her intentions were all but utter destruction of the land. 

Veyron renounces his faith and curses Vanos for betraying Columbia and being the cause for his soldiers demise. Vanos responds by summoning demons to murder his family and send him thru a multiverse portal to a random land, marked with a mark of pure evil to haunt him for the rest of his days. Veyron now finds himself in a new land, haunted by Vanos and all that happened to his family and soldiers….Now tasked with survival and a way to break the haunting and hopefully return to New BEUGHland and destroy Vanos once and for all. 

IMPORTANT NOTE: Vanos  is not a known deity in Elustria
