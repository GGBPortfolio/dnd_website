<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Timble comes from a family of nomads. He has been on the road since he was born, only ever stopping somewhere for a few months at a time. This lifestyle made adapting to most situations a breeze; All except one, the mystery of his past. Timble was always curious about his roots; Especially considering how prideful his people were when they discussed their goddess Akadi and the philosophy of nomadic life. However, anytime he would enquire about his past he was met with immediate stonewalling. Whether it was the group’s elders or his own parents, it seemed like many of the adults were adamant about not sharing their lore with the children of the convoy.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">He vaguely remembers another boy retelling a tall tale around the campfire about an impenetrable fortress. One with shifting metal walls, imprisoned magical beasts, and a devil child that runs the place. It was a story passed down from kid to kid, almost like a right of passage; However no one knew who started the story, or what inspired it. Timble didn’t think much of it until after he lost his parents. One of Timble's core memories was the last night they spent together when he was 12. The laughter, the games, the meal, and, of course, the one thing he had ever heard about his people’s past. His dad had said: That fortress brought winds not of Akadi’s will. Winds that destroyed our homeland. . . We barely escaped with our lives. . . Although your mother hushes up your drunken father, it was enough to plant a seed in your mind. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Timble stayed with the convoy for many years; Constantly asking the other adults about their “homeland” and what had happened the night of his parents’ death. Slowly but surely he was able to piece together that the convoy was actually on the run from another unknown group when the scouting party your parents were in was killed by a landslide. You begged and pleaded with the elders to discuss their history so that you may find some piece of mind, but they were stubborn. The youngest of the group’s leaders, Grudder von Goober, angrily told Timble to let his parents rest, and that pushed him to leave the convoy for good. He traveled Elustria on his own, jumping from cattle ranching job to farming job and so on; Never settling down, just like his parents had taught him. After a few years of this, he eventually heard of CROWN, a bounty collection agency looking for muscle. The pay was not as consistent, but the rewards were definitely more than he ever made. So he set forth to become a bounty hunter himself. </p>
