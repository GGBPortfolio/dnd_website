 <p style="text-indent: 2em;">Hack Sparrow was born and raised on the tippy tops of the giant trees in Maplesplint Woods (located west of Palomeras). The aarakocra are the only people that live in these parts. It is an inhospitable, wild land; At least while on the ground. The structures they built rest along the treetops of the 400-foot magical hyperions. This forest is the last of its kind outside of the designated “danger zones'' on the northern tips of either island. Hack is from Starmire, one of three treetop towns in Maplesplint Woods; All with their own governments and rivalries.  </p>
 <p style="text-indent: 2em;">Hack grew up around many aarakocra “dreaming” of joining Elustria’s military. The neighboring city of Malarpa is where many who want to join the royal guard are housed and trained until they are deployed. A lot of Hack’s family members, from his father to his distant cousins, are former or current military members. Although his family was initially supportive of his decision to not go into royal guard training, many were definitely upset by his desire for adventure. Hack would hang with Starmire hooligans and his father tried everything in his power to stop him. He would regularly get in trouble at the behest of his “gang leader” and good friend Crowley Thorn. Eventually many of his hooligan friends set out on their own adventures when they came of age, and so did he.</p>
