**Angus McCloud** is a creature not of the Mortal Realm. He comes from a plane called **The Shifting Acres**, an infinite stretch of wilderness ruled by **the Ents**. Fairy kind has co-inhabited this realm with the Ents since the beginning of time, or so he was told in the school he didn’t pay much mind to. Instead of focusing on his studies like his parents and head master wished, Angus spent his youth getting into trouble. Aside from the childish grifts and street performances, Angus had a real knack for seduction. It was almost as if McCloud was born knowing the sensual touch of a woman because that is all he focused on once he hit the ripe old age of 10\. He’d spend his nights out late, performing at taverns and hitting on the beautiful barmaids until he was finally at an age where they would welcome his advances. 

He spent many decades being a traveling performer. Going from one fairy village to the next, leaving behind adoring fans and love-struck groupies. Although he tried for many years to perform for the Ents, he was never able to get an audience with them. Contrary to what Angus had been taught at a young age, he had discovered that the relationship between the Ents and Fairies hadn’t been great since **the Druids** had fled from the Mortal Realm into theirs. Yet he was convinced that he could bridge that rift with his songs. He traveled to the capital of the fairy lands, **Sparkling Woods**, to ask their ruler for help. **Oleander MacBlubblesprout**, the king of the fairies, refused his pleas for talks between the Ents.

**“They chose their side. They don’t want nothin’ to do with us anymore.”**  

Angus felt defeated and, more importantly, insulted; His skills slept on by a bumbling monarch who knew nothing of romanticism. And due to his many years on the road as a *slutty bagpipe star*, he coped the best way he knew how. After blacking out the night before, Angus awoke to Oleander yelling at his guards to arrest him; Prying him from the naked queen’s embrace. The trial was short, and not structured in Angus’ favor. He was banished to the Mortal Realm, however their code of conduct worked a little differently than that of other cultures. Angus would always have the ability to ask for a single retrial as long as he was able to prove that he had gone through hardships and change. 

The silver fox was confident that he would be able to prove himself beyond a shadow of a doubt. That is, until he received a parting gift from the king he had slighted. As an extra precaution from a place of pure pettiness, Oleander had his best mages place *a curse* on Angus. Once he had entered the Mortal Realm through **a Rift**, a mystical portal between realms, he would forget where it was. He was cursed to know that there was a way back home, but unable to find it ever again. It has been 3 years since he was banished from his homeland. Angus has managed to learn about Elustria and mortal life in his travels, especially how rare his kind is there. He has been able to sustain himself by performing, betting, seducing, and grifting. However, he knew that if he were ever able to go back to The Shifting Acres, he would not be able to prove himself to his peers living like this. So he turned to the only accessible form of honor, wealth, and glory these lands provided to any willing participant: bounty hunting.
