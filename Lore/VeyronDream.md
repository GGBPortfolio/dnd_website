You emerge from the fog in between opaque pieces of fabric. As you brush them away you realize they are the curtains of your villa. You look out the balcony of your old home overlooking the glistening capital of Beughland. The wind brushes the curtain back in front of you, and when they clear once more you see the city you fought for now in ruins. Being burned and plundered by the legions of Hell. Vanos, your false deity, appears before you. Before they are able to take off the mask you had sworn for years was their face, you fall through the floor. You continue to fall through what appears to be the nine levels of hell. It is all indistinguishable save for another masked figure. This one with three faces: a neutral, a happy, and a sad face. You are barely able to distinguish anything else before you are swallowed up by the pages of a giant book.

You suddenly wake up.
