**Semaj Yak** was born and raised in the small village of **Obiwame**. East of **Frostwing Keep**, somewhat up the **Shilu Mountains**. You remember a time where your village was secluded, away from politics and war. Your culture consisted of proving yourself in brave and heroic ways. For the boys and men, glory through combat was the most accessible means of achieving status in **Obiwame**. He doesn’t remember much about his mother, she passed when he was only 4\. Unfortunately for Semaj, that meant that he was stuck with his father, **Goddwarr Yak**. An abusive, alcoholic warrior of great renown who’s cold, indifferent  demeanor shaped Semaj into the earth genasi he is today. 

**Semaj** was trained in combat since he was very young by his dead-beat dad. It wasn’t until he was 11 that he began picking fights around town, beating even some adults. It was around age 13 when things in the village started to change. The borders that **Lord Einar of Avilon** had a decent grip on were slowly pushed up the mountains until **Obiwame** was engulfed. **Einar** has surrounded the mountains because the **Shilu Shogunate** still reside within them. They refuse to bow to anyone, and although the territory belongs to **Lord Einar**, they remain one of the few places in Elustria that actively renounce the crown. What they are doing now remains a mystery to all.

Now that the village was forced under the boot of a tax-collecting outsider, many of the old heads opposed and deserted; **Goddwarr Yak** was one of the first, leaving **Semaj** behind. The village elders did their best to raise **Semaj** to conform to their new lifestyle, but he could not go back. The second he turned 18 years of age, **Semaj Yak** set off in search of glory. To become a hero worthy of returning to a town that had been neutered, in hopes of revitalizing the culture that he was raised in.
