The royal plaza is full with all kinds of people; Families and couples, young and old, with a plethora of mercenaries and rugged types waiting in anticipation. You manage to push your way through the crowd until you get to a fountain with enough room to stand up on its edge. Kids play in the water while teenagers climb the majestic statue in the middle; A young King Barrett slaying a massive, scary Druid warrior. You notice as the crowd starts to cheer when a plume of smoke rises from the castle walls in front of them. As the clouds grow over the crowd, a face is projected within it. A middle aged, bearded man in elegant clothes, fine jewelry, and spectacles befitting a scholar. Some of the folk around you identify him as **Lord Einar the Clever**, ruler of **Avilon**. Some express surprise at his appearance, expecting their beloved king to speak to them.

**“Welcome, one and all!!!”** He speaks, his voice echoes in the minds of all in the plaza. **“I have some somber news to begin this decree. . . King Barrett is dead. . .”**

The crowd erupts in a myriad of reactions. Whispers of gossip and theory, cries of agony and pain, but mostly looks of concern and shock exchanged amongst one another. All wondering what comes next for Elustria.

**“I know this will come as a shock to you all. I am here to give you the news of his passing. . . about a month ago, our beloved ruler, Otto Barrett, unifier of the old houses, herald of peace and prosperity, was murdered in his bed chamber. . .”** Einar pauses to give the crowd a moment to react, like a comedian pausing for laughter or a bard for applause. **“Many of you have not traveled here from far and wide to hear this, but it pains me to say that it is related to his untimely end. . . A bounty was posted around the same time. One that has shaken this nation to its core, and has rattled the best of the best to come forth and seek their fortune. . .  As the head of CROWN’s Intelligence Department, I am here to announce that we believe Melville Barrett, the quarry in question, to be responsible for King Barrett’s death. . .”**

Once excited mercenaries, laughing and drinking in anticipation, now stand there silently; Completely engrossed as they witness history unfold before them. Kids watch some of their parents cry, unable to comprehend their reactions. 

**“We believe that Melville Barrett is accompanied by two co-conspirators. Their motives are unknown as of now. The Librarians are working diligently to understand this crime and to bring all of you bounty hunters the best information we can. . . This is to say that some information is still being uncovered and we will likely have more to offer in the next four days at all CROWN offices.”** Einar gives another pause, but quickly returns when he hears the disgruntled cries of all the bounty hunters in the crowd. The eye rolling is palpable. **“The reason we are postponing the release of more information is not only to avoid sending you fine men and women on a wild goose chase, it is also to announce your NEW KING\!\!\!”**  

There is a long pause. Einar clears his throat, and it echoes in the silence of the plaza.

**“Here, in this plaza\! THREE DAYS TIME\!\!\! Come one, come all to see your new ruler crowned at the end of the night as we spend the day celebrating and mourning King Otto Barrett. As the pre-paid celebrations go on out here, the Royal Court members and their many distinguished guests will come together within the castle walls to discuss the future of this country with our new ruler.”** Einar notices that the crowd of armed individuals are still not satiated. He clears his throat once more and pulls on his tight, collared shirt. **“As we announce our new king, we shall also pass out any reliable information we have gathered in the form of a new WANTED POSTER for Melville Barrett seeing as now the bounty has been raised to 100 MILLION PLATINUM PIECES\!\!\! . . . Enjoy your stay in Barronia just a little while longer, for the floodgates will soon be opened. . .”**

The projection in the sky disappears and the smoke slowly dissipates overhead. The crowd lingers in confusion as they all talk to one another about what had just transpired.

**“Why do I feel blue-ballsed? . . .”** You hear one merc say to another.

**“That’s probably cause you were. . . Damn I was packed and ready to leave immediately.”**

**“Meh, at least we get some free food and festivities out of it.”**

**“Wish we could get into that private, fancy party before the announcement. . .”**
