You manage to sneak away to the outer edges of the enormous palace. Sneakily avoiding the guards on patrol. Eventually you make your way to a giant door that leads into a hexagonal room. There looks to be a guard station there, but the chair has collected dust.

As you enter this room, you notice that the floor and ceiling are just reflective enough to create the effect of an infinite tower going up and down. Every detail is the same, it even ripples of the glass with every step you take. As you inspect the room further, you notice that each of the other walls have their own giant doors; These ones closed. You approach one of them and you notice that there is a mechanical device next to them. It is a metal cylinder system with three alternating digits on them that represent a number. Next to it is a lever. You check behind you before opening the door and entering Room 972\.  

 	You enter a fancy foyer styled like a Victorian Era waiting room. You inspect the dusty items for a bit before moving on to the rest of the mysterious apartment. It takes you a while to search through the entirety of this place. It has all the amenities a royal family could want. A nursery, servants’ quarters, a green room, a library; All with well-fabricated images and lighting of the outdoors peering through the bolted-shut windows. After spending some time searching for anything of value, you realize that this place had been cleaned out a long time ago. So you decide to head back out to the hexagonal room you came from and try another one of the numbered doors; This time 619\.

	When you open this door, you are surprised to see the same things. The foyer, the nursery, the servants’ quarters, the green room, the library; All identical to the apartment you had just inspected. And just like the last one, it has been wiped clean but much more recently than the last place. No cobwebs and layers of dust caked on each and every surface. Anything of value is gone, save for a baby’s rattle you see underneath the dining room table. 

As you are about to leave Room 619, you hear voices coming from the hexagonal room. You peer through the crack in the giant door and see three men coming out of one of the doors you hadn’t yet inspected. All three Legionnaires, two of them whipping blood off of their blades while the other pushes a wheelbarrow with a tarp over it. Engraved on the wooden wheelbarrow it reads “Heartholm Supplies.” As he slowly squeaks it across the floor towards the exit, one of the others gets angry at him and points out that blood is spilling onto the floor from under the tarp. They get into an argument and eventually leave to find someone to clean up the mess. You step back out into the mirror room once the coast is clear and you hesitantly peer underneath the tarp. You see servants, bloody and maimed. Before you decide to head back to the party so as to not get caught, you catch a glimpse of the apartment the three soldiers had just come from; Room 209\. The furniture is tossed and there are blood streaks in the foyer.
