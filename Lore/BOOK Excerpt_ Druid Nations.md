TITLE: **Druid Nations: A Dissection of our Greatest Fear**  
AUTHOR: **Dr. Ingrid Saltevastan**

**DAY 1**  
This portion of the text goes over outsider theories on when and where the Druids came from. Some say they sprouted from the Earth like any other plant. Others say that their deities, Sylvanus and Chauntea, birthed them in order to provide the world with much needed balance. Regardless of the origin, the Druids played a huge part in the history of Old Elustria. Elustria was once called many things by many different factions, but Kodokuna was the one that stuck due to how influential they were. 

Many texts dating back centuries insinuate that the Druids were not people who just lived in thickets and swamps. They had their own cities and laws. A thriving culture that actually split into two separate communities. The author alludes to an inciting incident called the Great Splintering, and that the communities were split by the Connecting Isles; A different Druid nation on the eastern and western islands of Kodokuna.

**DAY 2**  
Although many of their spiritual and cultural practices were the same, both Druid Nations had one main difference; Their involvement with other people. The druids of the western island helped a lot of the clans who were willing in developing their infrastructure and agricultural practices. Although they tried imposing regulations as the clans gained more power and land, these druids saw themselves less as rulers and more as caretakers. To the author it is an undeniable fact that this Druid Nation helped shape the powerhouses we see today.

The druids of the eastern island took more of a hands-off approach to their neighbours. They kept to themselves in their Brosiel Forest strongholds. They were adamant on letting the clans and houses around them build and destroy as they pleased. This Druid Nation cared little for politics and uplifting the mortal tribes around them, but were no strangers to fighting back to save their lands. Although it is recorded that there were some interactions with the Shilu Shogunate, these druids’ ideologies were vastly different to their eastern counterparts. 

**DAY 3**  
This text goes into detail about the original three Druids of legend: **Nysoru, Guilpharess,** and **Callitris**. The druids believe in ancient beings called the **Kigimori** who have been here millenia before the dawn of the first man. In seeing the rise of the first mortals, the Kigimori attempted to create their own, resulting in the first three prophets. They were supposedly immortal and ever-youthful, but the author notes that they began to reincarnate into other druids over time; She states that whatever happened between the Immortal Three and these reincarnations is something she was unable to find anything on.

**DAY 4**  
The difference in approach towards other mortals seemed to come from a story related to the Three Child Prophecy. The split came after a major conflict took place between the reincarnations of the three prophets. After having warned some of the mortal clans to take a more “conservationist approach” to their expansion, some of them took to hunting Druid children in retaliation. This caused an argument between the three prophets of the time, with one of them leading a similar retaliation. This prophet went on a rampage that engulfed Kodokuna, setting back the work of his predecessors for generations. After the other two prophets were able to defeat him, this led to what is known as the Great Splintering.

The series of events leading to the deaths of two prophets left the Druid community in shambles. They couldn’t agree on whether to continue guiding mortals or to take a more isolationist approach. Some thought that the best course of action would be to show them how to coexist with nature. Others believed that they had incurred their wrath by pushing against the mortals’ nature. The Great Splintering was essentially a debate on whether or not mortals could change their ways. This event was marked by members of their court initiating a mass migration of sorts. Those who believed that the mortals were not their “pet project” moved to Brosiel Forest to establish their own communities.

**DAY 5**  
After the Great Splintering, the birth of the prophets was no longer the joyous moment it had been in the past. Now a prophet could be a terrible omen; A plague on the world. Although they expected great things from these druids, communities would not celebrate them like they used to in fear of jinxing it. Much was done to ensure that suspected child prophets were well accommodated and educated. Elders would gather and perform blessing rituals on suspected prophets for good luck. They would even go as far as housing entire families in luxurious common spaces so as to not uproot the children too abruptly. This eventually led to a better educational system within the druid communities.

**DAY 6**  
The Druid Nations, although split, would still do a lot together. They would have the same festivals and holidays, coordinate defensive forces along their territories, and trade via a long, lost route called the Moon Highway. Everything was done with careful consideration as to respect one another’s ideologies after the Great Splintering; However, the differing communities did struggle with one thing in particular: **the prophets**. 

They could agree on some things like the special treatment of suspected prophets and raising them to prevent future repeats. However, the ideology taught to said druid prophets was different depending on which side they found themselves on. The western island druids wanted a nurturing, welcoming figure that would work tirelessly to guide the mortals to a more conservationist lifestyle. The eastern island druids wanted a strong figure that would help strengthen Druid communities, and protect the wildlands of Kodokuna with firm boundaries and practices. After a few iterations of the reincarnations and high tensions, some supposedly leading to fighting, the prophets of the time devised a plan to even the playing field: To split them amongst communities.

**THE REST OF THE BOOK IS BURNED.**
