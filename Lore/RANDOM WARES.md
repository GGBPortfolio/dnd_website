**BARGAIN BIN**  
For 30 gold a pop, you may grab a single, unmarked bottle from a barrel full of mystery vials and bottles.  The DM will roll or use his “potion generator” to see what you pick. **You get the  description of the bottle and liquid inside, but not its effect**. You get one chance to pick another after you have heard the description.
