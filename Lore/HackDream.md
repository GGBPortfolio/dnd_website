As you cross the fog you find yourself disoriented; Almost as if you are about to lose your balance. You come to on the deck of a ship as the faceless crew fights for its life in the middle of a thunderstorm. Now the feeling of disorientation makes sense as the ships bobs violently on the massive waves. Water pours on the deck, men fly from one side to the other, and the lightning illuminates the sails as it pierces the black clouds.  

A crudely red bird is shown on the sail for that brief second of light in the the violent darkness. The captain stands beside you. Although you cannot make them out, you can sense a childhood friend. You watch them bark orders as a wave the size of a mountain approaches the bow of the ship.

A choir of whispers begins to drown out the sounds of the storm as the tidal wave approaches. Right before you engulfed by the dark waters, you see a pair of giant, yellow eyes peer at you from within the wave.

You suddenly wake up.
