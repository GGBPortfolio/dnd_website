Edwin,

I would prefer if we have these sorts of discussions in person. I am not a fan of having the paper trail for these things.

Once you are back in town we can discuss the matter of the death reading. If it hasn't been changed yet, you will immediately want to make it a private meeting so that no other attendees can be in the room for when I bring you back briefly. After that you will have to give me a detailed list of subjects you would like to avoid telling the truth. 

Then there's the matter of my limitations. As long as I am well compensated, your wife can remember you as a faithful man. However, if the law ever asks the question, I will be telling the truth. I will not be risking my neck to hide your past deeds, regardless of the guilt you feel. And if you bring up my past deeds as a way to convince me to bend the rules, I will personally ask the Constabulary to bbe present for your confessions.
