Lady Arabella,

It has become increasingly harder to not revisit that night we spent together ferrying from Hearholm to Avilon. Sometimes I imagine I feel your touch as I work late nights. The smell of your perfume haunts me as my wife has seen to have taken a liking to the same brand. I wish to see you again.

I will be leaving Avilon once more to close a deal on a new team. Let us meet again at the Three Shadows. Maybbe we can recount that night once more. 

Sincerely yours,

**Edwin**
