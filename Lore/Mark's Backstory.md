<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Zelicek Kral and his parents were part of a traveling group of devil-hunting monks known as Hellions’ Bane. One fateful day, his mother was killed during an ambush led by Xaniax, an infamous goblin war chief. After the attack, his father took a vow of silence and stayed in the part of the woods where she was killed. The group respected his wishes, seeing as vows like these were common practice, and so Zelicek was left to tend to him as they moved on. Ever since he was 7 years old he cared for his father, and learned how to survive off the land for both of their sakes. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Fast forward to when he was 12, a strange man noticed him on the side of the road where he meditated everyday. The stranger lashed out at the man as you were returning from the woods with food. Seeing Zelicek made the stranger even angrier, and it prompted him to start beating his father. Zelicek tried to protect him, and the sadistic man turned his anger towards him. He proceeded to gouge out Zelicek’s eyeballs right in front of him as if he knew the father wouldn’t lift a finger. He did sit motionless as his son cried out for help. The man left him alive to hear him kill his father; His screams were the first time Zelicek had heard him speak in years. There are only two things Zelicek remembers about this man: the black and crimson armor he bore under his robes, and the last words he spoke in his presence. </p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em; font-weight: bold;">“A fine pair you have, boy. . . I’m sorry I must take ‘em. . .”</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">He was able to survive due to the kindness of a local woman named Ethel who found him on the road. After he had recovered, he trained every day in the woods and would only come back to the woman’s house to sleep. Every night, he would hear the gentle pitter-patter of mice on the cold ground. He eventually befriended one of them, Pooter, who would go out on hunts with him; They were inseparable. Eventually, after 6 long years of training, he and Pooter set out into the world to find the man responsible for his maiming. Ethel gave him a Broken Seeker’s Compass so that he may find his way back to her once he’s finished his quest. It has been 2 years since you’ve left Ethel’s home in search for your father’s killer; Surviving off of the land and the money from bounties you were able to collect. </p>
