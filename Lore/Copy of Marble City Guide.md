# Marble City Guide

## Mordos’ Insights and Background

## General Info

Marble City was once the ancestral home of the Tantakhan Family. When the Elustrian Empire seized their homeland, the construction of the city began shortly after. The lords that ruled over the area transformed it into a luxurious destination for both high-born patrons and low life criminals. 

The city proper is separated by three main sections; The Polished District, the Gem District, and the Cobblestone District. The Polished District is where luxury homes and inns are located for high-born tourists and citizens. This area is heavily guarded and generally closed to the public. The Gem District makes up the bigger part of the “sunshine city.” This is where most of the attractions and shops are located. Lastly there is the Cobblestone District, or The Pits, a series of quarries where all other districts are suspended over. These quarries and abandoned mineshafts are home to shanty towns for those less fortunate. Although there are very few things of interest for tourists in the “sunless” city, the Pits are where a lot of the criminal element rests. This is where Mordos grew up.  

## Backstory

**Mordos Shadowstrike** was born and abandoned at an orphanage in the **Cobblestone District** of **Marble City**; A sprawling urban paradise for the rich with a massive criminal underbelly. He started off stealing the things he wanted from markets and wealthy passersby. One day he stole from someone who meant to place a curse on him as a consequence for his sticky fingers; Instead Mordos came face-to-face with a fiendish deity named **Glasya, daughter of Asmodeus**. At the ripe age of 10, Mordos made a pact with Glasya but doesn’t exactly remember what the deal was; He blames it on it having happened so long ago that he just doesn’t remember. He became a lot better at what he did and caught the eye of a big-time mob called **The Quarry**.

Over the years of working for a mystery boss, Mordos became increasingly better at his magical craft; trying his best to climb the ranks in hope of one day meeting, maybe even usurping, their leader: “**The Stonemason**.” However, things get complicated when he is assigned to a new squad after his previous one was annihilated by a group of demon worshippers The Stonemason wanted gone. This new “family” brought along a type of distraction Mordos had never anticipated before: love. He met **Lysandra**, a cunning young woman that had started working for The Quarry around the same time as Mordos. Although their skill sets and family lives were very different, they bonded and grew fond of one another. They fell madly in love. They would usually sneak away from their squad for passionate nights out on the town. However, things took a turn for the worst on one, fateful date night.

Mordos had saved up to bring Lysandra to a fancy restaurant she had always dreamed of going to called **The Nine Levels of Heaven**. He had reservations, but the night turned sour when the staff turned them both away for being “*cobblestone cur*.” In a moment of rage, his patron took advantage of his clouded judgment and unlocked Mordos’s hellish powers. It was a crime of passion, and they both fled the scene as the concierge, and most of the restaurant, went up in flames. **Lysandra** tried to keep quiet about it, even warning Mordos to never use that kind of magic again; Something he could get in a lot of trouble for with **The Quarry**. However, Mordos was too focused on making a name for himself. He used his new found abilities for a time, surprising his squad and getting the attention of The Stonemason himself. However, it was not the kind of attention that Mordos wanted. One night, Lysandra snuck into Mordos’s room with a packed bag and warned him that The Quarry were setting him up to fail on his next big heist. They were planning on turning him in as the man that burned down the restaurant to get in with the rich folk of Marble City. She wasn’t sure why, but The Stonemason had a thing for those **“touched by the Nine Hells.”** It broke Mordos’s heart that Lysandra couldn’t go with him, seeing as she had elderly parents that The Quarry provided for. He took what he could and he never set foot in Marble City again.

## Quarry Lore (**Based on when Mordos was here last**)

* **Quarry Ranking System**:   
  * **Shale**: Lowest rank in the Quarry, composed of any and all that want to join. Initiates running small tasks and odd jobs for anyone in The Quarry.  
  * **Limestone**: This rank is for those who have been deemed worthy to take part in the initiation process. You then would get placed into one of the other ranks and set up with a crew after being assessed.   
  * **Iron**: The muscle of the organization. This group is more involved in the brutality of syndicate life; turf wars, trafficking, stealing, break-ins, etc. Mordos was an Iron member.  
  * **Quartz**: These members tend to become apprentices on the business side of things. They run fronts, warehouses, and any businesses associated with the Quarry.  
  * **Marble**: Very few reach this status as they are members that relay messages to the Stonemason through his contacts (diamonds). A council of sorts composed of those who run entire sectors of the Quarry’s operations. One usually has to inherit this position through merit, and only on the approval of the rest. They relay information to the Irons and Marbles.  
  * **Diamond**: The close, personal guards of the Stonemason. These are the cream-of-the-crop, and the only ones that deal with him directly; Legends of the gang having risen above all else. They are the strongest of the ranks that accompany and protect The Stonemason or run special errands.

* **Initiation**: The process of initiating into the Quarry varies based on the skill set of the applicant. Irons usually go through excruciating physical tasks whereas Quartz tend to do more intellectual puzzles/tasks. Once a Limestone has passed their test, their initiation is marked by a high-ranking member collecting a memory of theirs. They’ll never tell the initiate what memory it is but are told that it is something that can be used against them in case they turn on the Stonemason.

* **Business Practices**: The Quarry have their hands in many ventures. Loan sharking, drug slinging, petty and corporate theft, but most importantly smuggling. The docks are their main source of income. They have points of contact with almost any shipping company and pirating group that docks in Marble City; If they don’t, they eventually will. 

* **Rivals and Allies**:   
  * **Oni Clan**: Long time enemies. They started off not being a problem, but have grown into a real competitor over time. The Stonemason and Diamonds were always asking about their movements.  
  * **Lord Brozzerious**: In pretty good standing with The Quarry. Several diamonds and marbles have been known to attend his parties and council meetings to represent the Stonemason’s interests.  
  * **Ivory Might**: A long time enemy of The Quarry. They are guerilla revolutionaries wanting to free Marble City from Lord Brozzerious and his ilk; They do not see eye-to-eye on the future of the city.    
  * **The Ichiban Dynasty**: One of the oldest allies they have. This pirate group is one of their biggest suppliers.   
  * **The Trenchers**: Another pirate group they associate with. This group mostly helps with supplying food and materials for their many endeavours.  
  * **Tantakhan Family**: The old guard of the city. This old house has always hated what Marble City stood for, and they HATE players like the Stonemason for it.  
  * **Palacio Di Sangue**: They have tried their hand in getting in on cooking the books with those that run the colosseum. Although they were never really successful, the owners appreciate the business and volunteers that the Quarry brings. 

## Locations

* **The Nine Levels of Heaven**: A high-end restaurant in the Polished District. Reservations need to be made months in advance. Their clientele are usually those who spend their days in the same district, but they make sure to have a section reserved for “others” if they even let them in.  
* **Briar’s Ship Shape Shoppe**: A warehouse located in one of the busiest harbors of Marble City. This place sells all kinds of goods to sailors and shipwrights, all while being a front for The Quarry. The owner, Frederick Briar, is the high ranking member who introduced Mordos to syndicate life.   
* **The Spittoon Swamp**: A pub in the Pits where Mordos’ team frequented. Many nights were spent here, not only for a good time but because the barkeep would give his boss many of their Quarry assignments.  
* **Anderson’s Home of the Houseless**: Mordos grew up in this shelter/orphanage located in the lower end of the Gem District. Father Anderson III tried his best to keep Mordos off the streets, but when he found out he had gone through with the Quarry’s initiation process, he kicked him out.   
* **Palacio Di Sangue**: One of the biggest spectacles in Marble City; a gladiator colosseum in the Gem District. Ticket prices vary depending on the events, but Mordos remembers going to a couple of these with Balthazaar and Donovan.  
* **Don Salvo’s**: A dance club in the Gem District that Mordos spent many nights coming out to with Lyssandra when they snuck away from the group.

## People

* **Balthazaar**: Leader of Mordos’ crew in the Quarry. Point of contact with the higher ups, usually came up with the plans. Last time you saw him he was significantly older than you (40s). He’s gruff, likes to fight, and would always have your back in a pinch.    
* **Donovan**: An Iron member, a little older than Mordos at the time (20s). Always by Balthazaar’s side trying to impress him. He and Mordos had a love/hate relationship, a brotherly rivalry.    
* **Coco**: the second oldest in the group, and Balthazaar’s right hand. She was the cool, tough aunt of the group; Imparting wisdom and beat downs to the younger members if need be. A life-long Iron with a knack for sneaking and safe-cracking.  
* **Lysandra**: Mordos’ long lost love, a fellow Iron of the same age. Cut from the same cloth, Mordos and Lysandra grew closer and closer the longer they worked under Balthazaar.  
* **Tarrik**: The muscle of the group. A giant, lovable goliath with a limited vocabulary. Although not the brightest bulb, he certainly knew how to protect the group when things got heated.  
* **Frederick Briar**: Owner of the Ship Shape Shoppe, he was the man that introduced Mordos to the Quarry lifestyle. He is a Quartz member who would give your crew the bigger gigs from time to time. He is the Quarry’s frontman for all dock-related business.  
* **Erin Knicknack**: bartender and owner of The Spittoon Swamp. Balthazar’s main point of contact for Quarry work.  
* **Darion Chokehold**: One of the few Diamonds of the Quarry. Mordos doesn’t know much about him; Only met him once when he was speaking to Balthazaar the week before he had to flee the city.   
* **Father Anderson III**: cleric that ran the orphanage Mordos grew up in. He is a cleric of renown in The Pits; Always helping those in need. Many would line up outside of his shelter for medical and spiritual help.  
* **The Stonemason**: the enigmatic leader of The Quarry. Supposedly not even the diamonds have seen his face. Many rumors around this person, and only if any dare to whisper his name.
