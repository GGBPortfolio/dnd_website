<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Mordos Shadowstrike was born and abandoned at an orphanage in the Cobblestone District of Marble City; A sprawling urban paradise for the rich with a massive criminal underbelly. He started off stealing the things he wanted from markets and wealthy passersby. One day he stole from someone who meant to place a curse on him as a consequence for his sticky fingers; Instead Mordos came face-to-face with a fiendish deity named Glasya, daughter of Asmodeus. At the ripe age of 10, Mordos made a pact with Glasya but doesn’t exactly remember what the deal was; He blames it on it having happened so long ago that he just doesn’t remember. He became a lot better at what he did and caught the eye of a big-time mob called The Quarry.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Over the years of working for a mystery boss, Mordos became increasingly better at his magical craft; trying his best to climb the ranks in hope of one day meeting, maybe even usurping, their leader: “The Stonemason.” However, things get complicated when he is assigned to a new squad after his previous one was annihilated by a group of demon worshippers The Stonemason wanted gone. This new “family” brought along a type of distraction Mordos had never anticipated before: love. He met Lysandra, a cunning young woman that had started working for The Quarry around the same time as Mordos. Although their skill sets and family lives were very different, they bonded and grew fond of one another. They fell madly in love. They would usually sneak away from their squad for passionate nights out on the town. However, things took a turn for the worst on one, fateful date night.</p>
<p style="text-indent: 2em;">Mordos had saved up to bring Lysandra to a fancy restaurant she had always dreamed of going to called The Nine Levels of Heaven. He had everything ready, even reservations, but the night turned sour when the staff turned them both away for being “*cobblestone cur*.” In a moment of rage, his patron took advantage of his clouded judgment and unlocked Mordos’s hellish powers. It was a crime of passion, and they both fled the scene as the concierge, and most of the restaurant, went up in flames. Lysandra tried to keep quiet about it, even warning Mordos to never use that kind of magic again; Something she could get in a lot of trouble for with The Quarry. However, Mordos was too focused on making a name for himself. He used his new found abilities for a time, surprising his squad and getting the attention of The Stonemason himself. However, it was not the kind of attention that Mordos wanted. One night, Lysandra snuck into Mordos’s room with a packed bag and warned him that The Quarry were setting him up to fail on his next big heist. They were planning on turning him in as they man that burned down The Nine Levels of Heaven as a ploy to get in with the rich folk of Marble City. She wasn’t sure why, but The Stonemason had a thing for those “touched by the Nine Hells.” It broke Mordos’s heart that Lysandra couldn’t go with him, seeing as she had elderly parents that The Quarry provided for. He took what he could and he never set foot in Marble City again.</p>
